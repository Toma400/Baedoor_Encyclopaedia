# 🧱 Conlang Neologisms' Guide
When creating new words, it's useful to follow some principles to ensure coherent feeling of
the language - especially if the language itself is not fully set in stone/complete.

### Advices
- consider (loosely) [Naming Schemes](Langue/Naming_Schemes.md)
- reason words from concepts, not translations - they should have meaning connected to culture
  and not come from position of "it is X translation of Y"
  - this helps with realistic "unique words" and forging words that have multiple meanings/
    nuanced interpretation without it feeling artificial - since the process of building it up
    comes from the understanding of people first
  - IRL languages were not built, so that dynamic came naturally; made languages tend to be built
    from the endpoint (translation language [English here] being one of them) making them feel off
  - saphtric idea of `taikan` is good example - it could be translated roughly to "solidarity"
    but it has ties to the understanding of society by saphtri, not direct English word, the
    translation occurs as post-thought

### Suggestions
- use portmanteaus (combination of two letters) to form combined meaning
