# 📓 Southern Islands
**Author**: [Naevin Manaut](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Manaut_Naevin.md)  
**Published**: 415 NE, Sargin University Press (East Baedoor, Sargin)

---
Book on southern cultures of **Baedoor Continent** inhabiting islands of Kaer and Arennan.  
The work was written with pop-scientific approach to be rather accessible than in-depth, as stated
by the author, its goal was to rather be informative and inspiring than particularly academic.

### Writing commentary
Written through the lens of baedoorian scholar, it yields common tropes of the culture, such
as national pride and judgements made from perspective of past imperial state.  
That said however, him noticing goblins and portraying them as primitive race shows some nuance
absent among other baedoorian scholars who would rather omit their existence or portray them as wild
animals.
