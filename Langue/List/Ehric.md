# 🪔 Ehric
Ehric language was used by Ehrans, civilisation living on surface of Seiteh before Ghlodds migrated
and conquered this continent.   
It used single letters, making itself distinct from ghloddish heavily basing itself on double-letters.
Due to its weird structure and little remaining source texts, Ehric is considered as dead language, with
little to no chance to be revived.
