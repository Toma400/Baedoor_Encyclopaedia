## Letters
In First Era Ghloddish, there are relatively small amount of letters, compared to later eras.
They are also splitted into three categories, used in phonetic part of the language.

|                            |                  Letters                   |
|----------------------------|:------------------------------------------:|
| Doubled Consonants         | th, gh, sh, oe, oa, ar, ah, at, ta, to, te |
| Early Consonants (Endings) |                   -r, -n                   |
| Vowels                     |                 e, o, a, s                 |

## Pronunciation Rules
In First Era Ghloddish, there wasn't a lot of clear pronunciation rules, which wouldn't be
almost-hearable differences for foreigners.
