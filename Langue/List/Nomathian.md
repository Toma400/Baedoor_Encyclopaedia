# 🪔 Nomathian
Nomathian is language native for nomath civilisation, living on Baedoor lands before jitadish conquerors
came. Their language is rather unknown, but some jitadish scholars tried to recover it after a lot of
nomathian culture disappeared.  
Their language is known for rustly sound, which was often point of baedoorian jokes.
