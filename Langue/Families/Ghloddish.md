# ⛩️ Ghloddish
Ghloddish is one of the oldest languages in Baedoor universe, being used mainly by ghlodds. It is
considered partly as magical language too, though this is statement mainly on its first era, in which
a lot of magical aspects were still existing.

### Ghloddish history
Even though ghloddish evolution is pretty straight-lined and has not really much influences (letting it
remain quite unique throughout all that time), there are several influences which eventually led
ghloddish to become broader, as well as more accessible for usual speaker.

<center><b>Ghloddish evolution tree</b></center>

```
Ghloddish (I era) ------> Ghloddish (II era) ------> Ghloddish (III era)
                     ↑                                       |
                   Ehric                                     ├─────────── Global influences
                                                             ↓
                                                     Ghloddish (IV era)
```

Fourth era is not always recognised as new one, and a lot of ghloddish language purists say that this
is just "awful trend". It is, though, becoming too large to be objectively portrayed as anything
less than new era for ghloddish language.
