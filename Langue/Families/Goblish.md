### Short and long words
Goblin speak can be roughly separated into two word groups: short and longer ones. The distinction
isn't that clear, but in general shorter words tend to symbolise verbs and action-based concepts,
whereas longer ones conclude complex topics such as feelings and values.  
Contrary to the intuition, technicals and material aspects also tend to fall under shorter words,
but that also depends on context.

Goblin speak in this sense is somewhat agglutinative (long words can be built out of short ones,
similarly to how goblin art-script is done) but it's not as direct as with human languages, making
goblin speak very difficult to discern via rule-based learning.
