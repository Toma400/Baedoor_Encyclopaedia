# 🪔 Nomathian
Nomathian is language family native for nomathians, human branch that live on northern half of [East Moon](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon.md),
[Kaer](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer.md) and lived on [Baedoor Island](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent.md)
before jitadish conquerors.  
With such far spread of their cultures, nomathian language differs heavily between Moon inhabitants and
[kaerians](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/Kaerians/[Culture].md).

Their language is known for rustly sound, which was often point of baedoorian jokes.
