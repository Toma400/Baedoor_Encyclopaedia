# ⛩️ Trish
Tri (or trish, trianish) language is one of the oldest and yet, the most broadly used language in Baedoor universe.  
Unlike others, though, it relies on so many dialects and variants that it is difficult to really
capture its "universal essence", although some attempts are made under "general trish" name.

### Some grammar rules
`-i`/`-ai`/`-āī` suffix is usually used for diminutives or essentiality  
`-n` is usually used with adjectives as a suffix

`Lyrensi Purpui` means `lyre + ensi + purpue[--i]`
