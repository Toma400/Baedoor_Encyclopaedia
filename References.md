## Cameos

### Dylan
[Delai Eir](Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Eir_Delai.md), a young painter known for his artistic
skills among West Baedoor boheme.

Because well, his pixel art skills are just so good.

### Lyof
[Xaine](Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Xaine.md), oneironaut traveller.

The traveller of dreams and oververse and the mythical figure behind origins of 
oververse / multiverse research. Because of how much influence Lyof had on Baedoor's cosmology 
and how much our ideas intersected.

### Sajevius
[Say'ye'ys](Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Say'ye'ys.md), green dragon lying near Ghenash'r city
in the big cave the city is located, its guardian and patron.

Because of Sajevius' green dragon avatar.

### Tammilisäke
[Terska](Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Terska.md), kaikhi, kehtic diplomat.

Idea behind him being an arrogant goober who is silly enough to not realise being seen as less
important than he thinks of himself comes from Tammi using English word that had two meanings, one
of which was equivalent to "the fool".

### Toma400
[Tom Varteford](Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Varteford_Tom.md), a baedoorian king a bit too keen
on trying to be unproblematic.

An old self-insert that was rewritten to poke myself on one of my big flaws and in the same time
make the character more believable.

### Xeno
[Keno Nira](Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Nira_Keno.md), erdic scientist, astronomist and
librerian, lone wolf teaching sometimes on University of Illusion.

Just because it checks out all the hobby Xeno has lol.
