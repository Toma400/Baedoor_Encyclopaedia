# 🏹 Wastelands of Baedoor
**Wastelands of Baedoor** is name of Minecraft mod in 2021, introducing player to many new game
elements that were loosely connected with Baedoor.  
At some point (version v0.a10) it introduced the heaviest story-driven element that had
profound influence from this universe: ormath civilisation. This brought the mod closer to be
valid representation of Baedoor, and thus, is considered as one of official works taken in
context of the universe.

### MECHANICS
WoB was meant to be mod that adds firearms and renewability, later also steampunk elements to the
game. Most of those bear little to no connection to Baedoor RPG System, but the important aspect
that BRPGS takes from WoB is firearms mechanics - especially the idea of them being jammed.  
While Minecraft is game that does not tolerate complex mechanics easily, WoB system seemed to be
simple enough to not burden the game heavily, but complex enough to fit being remade even for more
demanding RPG experience.
