# 💰 Cheats
Between Shadows and Light has cheat system, allowing you to get items for free or visit
new locations.

### Reaching new locations
At the start of the game, if you type `cheat` when talking with captain, you will be transported
to [abandoned island](BSaL_Locations.md#abandoned-island-supcheatsup).

### Money and teleport `OG / RE`
Entering `cheat` in main game menu in remake will give you 150 coins and teleport to Evros 
Docks - which can be useful particularly to skip the tutorial sequence.  
In OG, that cheat would show you list of all items with detailed information, providing you
sort of guide on hidden statistics. That function was removed in remake, however.

### Combat cheats `RE`
In a remake you can also cheat your way out of the combat - entering `heal` in combat menu will
regenerate your health to full, and `kill` will kill the enemy in the same turn.
