# 🏹 Baedoor Classic
**Baedoor Classic** is name for merged content of notebook games I [explained in Baedoor section](/Baedoor.md#-trinity).
As they do have similar character, I see no point at differentiating their content.  
Important disclaimer though, this page here is maintained only for lore purposes, as any mechanics
or any other informations here are not likely to be reconstructed due to their fluidity.

### MECHANICS
Baedoor game made in 2008 used first iteration of Baedoor RPG System.

### LORE
Lore from notebooks is fairly incoherent with latter revisions, as they took place in 1008-1011 OE, 
yet it also had aspects recognised now as happening in different time period. This mainly affects
first Baedoor game incarnation, so this disclaimer is less heavy on Lands games, yet still
persists.  
This is also one of the reasons why this section is here - it is to store all variants of lore,
whether they are coherent nowadays or not.  
**Loreum** part is, in the other hand, official revisioned summary, which has priority regarding 
"true lore".
