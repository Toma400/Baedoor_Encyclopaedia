        page. Which tells us that at one point in time it was a very tiny hamlet, and since the

        occupying Empire.<br>
        The settlement itself shows us a bit differently looking guards (with red on their
        armour nudging the fact there could be unique style for armies conquering foreign lands).
        Names analysis - Olven is the obvious baedoorian name. I feel a bit less obvious about

### Lore accuracy
Very high lore accuracy, with only minimal nitpicks that do not follow established canon or broader
economic sense. So most notably the weird 1096 (A.D.?) date, economy built on diamonds and
transformations of coal to these.
Few renames were done to ensure more creative feel to what was mostly generic region naming.
