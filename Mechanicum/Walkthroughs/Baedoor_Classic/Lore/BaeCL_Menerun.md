### Lore accuracy
What is pretty surprising about `L.007` is lore coherency. While character names tend to be
a bit too random (or rather, visibly inspired by games I played at the time),
names of cities and general shapes of architecture tend to be rather closer to what current
image of Jitado says.  
Since the country didn't change *that* much over the years, it basically means the notebook could
be easily placed in any timespan and not be too odd for lore coherency.  
But since I do want to give myself a bit of freedom, let's say that it will take place in either
late PE or early OE, so I can shape late OE/NE Jitado later, if there's any need to emphasize
obvious technological advancements.  
It also makes sense considering this notebook came before Baedoor, and in a way, Baedoor notebook
started Old Era as a phenomenon separated from old notebooks.

So, we could sum it up in points:
- Names of this notebook vary, from very lore-friendly (Silas, Menerun) to not at all (Yulin-Hai)
- Architecture image is mostly canon, however very simplified
  - Small details, such as preference towards blue roofs instead of orange could be mentioned
- General coherence is rather high
