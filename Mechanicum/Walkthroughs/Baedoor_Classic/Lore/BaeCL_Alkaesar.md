### Lore accuracy
As far as my overview goes, `L.008` seems to be quite accurate with even newest lore towards Vershaton,
with only few nitpicks here and there. It also has an interesting difference with old planning that
I think would be useful to change in favour of the notebook - being Al Khasar position on the map.  
In old Baedoor map, the city is quite inland, while the notebook puts it next to the lake. This could
make an interesting reasoning behind this place being bigger and more important, especially if the
passage between Rakonnd and Vershaton is tight enough to still make people going through be able to
branch out to Al Khasar.

My nitpicks are related primarily to architecture, with minor ones towards names and fauna/flora:
- Desert areas seem to be represented *always* with pueblo architecture, and this doesn't
  exclude `L.008` which also used this trope; current planning has very different idea on
  Vershaton building style
  - That said, town of Radpat surprisingly follows much better (though simplified) depiction
- Names used across the notebook seem random, so they should be filtered through conlang lens
