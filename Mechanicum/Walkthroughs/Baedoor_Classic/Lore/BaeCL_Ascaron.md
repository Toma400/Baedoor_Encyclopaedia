        First panel opening Ascaron is somewhat surprising - it isn't the city itself, but its
        outskirts, with an iron mine and teleport. There isn't much happening on the page, but
        it does showcases the general themes I imagine Ascaron should include - especially if
        we went with more "small polities" theme for latoka. I imagine Ascaron would hold influence
        over emerald hills, thefore making mining as important part of its identity.
    </td>

  <tr>
    <td width="420" rowspan="2"><img alt="Egypt Colony Archives, Photo 15" src="/Assets/Sources/L_010_Egypt_Colony/arv_rossevette_14.jpg" width="416" height="307" /></td>
    <th align="centre">Ascaron Gates</th>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
        Ascaron fully greets us on the next page, where we are entering the city proper through
        its gates. While I assume the notebook was done with the idea of baedoorian occupation
        timeframe, I think retaining the massive walls can be interesting idea even for post-treaty
        time.<br>
        The gates are followed by the house of a bit unusual shape. I think it could work really
        nice as one of the coastal architecture sets for latoka, for rural/poorer use. It looks
        like it's made out of clay, even though the notebook claims it is built out of something
        named as "yellow" which makes the building invisible at night? I think there could be
        some sort of material that the buildings are made of, maybe some mixture of clay or a dye
        that makes the buildings... maybe not invisible, but have other useful property.<br>
        The page ends with some common vegetables grown, as a proof of Inluern being a farmer on
        top of food seller. The name also feels baedoorian enough that I decided to go with it
        being of mixed origins.
    </td>
  </tr>

  <!--PHOTO 3-->
  <tr>
    <td width="420" rowspan="2"><img alt="Egypt Colony Archives, Photo 16" src="/Assets/Sources/L_010_Egypt_Colony/arv_rossevette_15.jpg" width="416" height="307" /></td>
    <th align="centre">The Pyramid</th>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
        Next panel greets us with the view of morning time, but also a grand monumental pyramid.
        It strikes very bluntly that "Egypt Colony" notebook is inspired by irl Egypt, and the
        whole idea of latoka selling souvenirs only adds to that.<br>
        Not really much to be said here except that the building reminds me of irl Greek temples
        and could be seen as similar influence of Egypt vision during its conquest. It can work
        however for the latoka tomb/temple structure that we will see later again (albeit
        somewhat different, it strikes enough resemblances to just be considered its variant).<br>
        I imagine there could be an interesting hook to latoka using their culture to get 
        tourism going, at least in places that are more peaceful and richer in history. 
        Upon worldbuilding process, Ascaron ended up being a city with quite long history, 
        so this would be quite fitting purpose for the place.
    </td>
  </tr>
