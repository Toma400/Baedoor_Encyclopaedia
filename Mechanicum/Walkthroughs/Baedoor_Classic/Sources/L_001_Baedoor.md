### Description
Baedoor notebook is main source of knowledge on Baedoor Island lore, as it is the first and the last
such extensive notebook on that matter (in size, it is also one of the biggest notebooks of all, including
several cities and villages altogether).  

Additionally, there is also pretty big book list on its back, albeit heavily varied in lore-friendliness
due to being written in weird period of time.  
Lore-friendliness of the notebook itself is also very mixed, as it does follow correct patterns, but
does mix a lot of elements in loose way - making it, eventually, really incoherent within either
late lore (when a lot of aspects of universe was set up and re-aligned) or even early lore (when I
just didn't care about some incoherencies).   
Original premise was that the notebook sort-of started during Baedoor Empire conquests, therefore
next notebooks showed conquered countries, such as Kaer or Arennan. It did mix though with my roleplay
on Tom Varteford persona, who got later its important position in Baedoor lore itself.  
The main culprit is incoherent dating at the time, which made Tom Varteford exist in three planes:
- extremely long conquest era (when no notebook aspects were changed to adjust from early OE to late OE)
- originally stated year being absurdly late (when neither conquests nor Tom being adventurer could
  happen)
- roleplay context, stating Tom having adventures years before second plane, and living centuries after
  first one

This makes this notebook yet extremely valuable, but historically broken, so to speak. To see further
treating, look at [source lore accuracy](L_001_Baedoor.md#source-lore-accuracy) section. Either
way, this notebook began something which we could call "middle-era" of current Baedoor universe, and
in strict sense, the universe began with it, getting its own, proud name inherited from those lands.

### Source lore accuracy
Baedoor captured in this notebook is most incoherent of sourced materials, as it does have
several conflicting aspects with proclaimed time period (1008-1011 OE), namely:
- Tom Varteford being an adventurer, not yet king (which would apply to 966-998 OE)
- Baedoor looking as it would look in 200-300 OE, in most favourable view, 500 OE, but nowhere near
  said timeframe
- Isuga being desert city, whereas it always stayed on grasslands

But, in the same time, it is considered as core resource for lore exploration.  
Hence, sources on Baedoor from this notebook are treated in specific scope:
- Coherent aspects are used in lore
- Incoherences related to historical events and climate are ignored (knowing my mindset from times of
  drawing those lands)
- Incoherences related to architecture are used in lore accordingly to timeframe (so if architecture
  follow 200 OE timeframe, it will be used as source for this period)
- Kel Desert is moved onto Vershaton territory
