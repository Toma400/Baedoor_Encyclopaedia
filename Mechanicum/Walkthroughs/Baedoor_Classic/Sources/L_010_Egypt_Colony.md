### Description
If I recall correctly, Egypt Colony was one of the earlier Baedoor notebooks to date, and it had
much more adventure-driven character than previous RPGish iterations (this is even more emphasized
by second Rossevette-aligned notebook, L.011).

Generally speaking, Egypt Colony was built on, as the name itself implies, making civilisation
heavily built on egyptian theme. Hard for me to tell if it was Indiana Jones inspiration both
aspects combined, or something that developed independently, but the case is that there are many
parts the inspirations can be felt. That said, it's still quite unique depiction that mixes some
very different cultural things (onion architecture, baedoorian occupation, magic tech) to make
latoka and Rossevette distinct. I'm also surprised how much these pieces can be analysed outside
of other notebooks/lore, making it very independent and easier to align with whatever canon exists
currently.

Albeit there's no official dates on when the notebook was created, I found "latest update" note
on data notebook to contain `23.08.09`, which means it was likely to be made in 2009
(and certainly not later).

### Source lore accuracy
Very high lore accuracy, with only minimal nitpicks that do not follow established canon or broader
economic sense. So most notably the weird 1096 (A.D.?) date, economy built on diamonds and
transformations of coal to these.
Few renames were done to ensure more creative feel to what was mostly generic region naming.
