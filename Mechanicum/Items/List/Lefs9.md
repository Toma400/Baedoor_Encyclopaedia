### DESCRIPTION  
Lefs 9 has quite unfortunate history of being next revolver model proposed by **Lefs** company,
but due to specific request given by Baedoor Empire, the design of it was flawed enough to not
get popularity even within the army itself.  
While the revolver does its work well and does not bear any technical problems often seen in other
guns (such as jamming), it is rather slow and has annoying form of reloading, which makes it
rarely liked among shooters.  
Despite that, officers sometimes still wear Lefs 9 revolvers with them, and several of those
guns can be found also on foreign lands, being sold more cheaply than popular models.
