### DESCRIPTION  
Ayer shotgun is name on primitive family of shotguns made by jitadian bandits (hence the name being
translated literally as *bandit shotgun* in jitadian). It has several variants and can be customised
heavily - which is one of reasons why they got so popular despite bad quality and execution.
It is also pretty lightweight.

Ayer shotguns raw power is also something to comment - while they often jam and can even hurt the
user, shooting power can take out heavy beast or human in one shot, if you get lucky.  
Combined with customisability that is very easy on this type of firearm, skilled gunsmith can
convert this awkward gun into a killing machine.

Ayer shotguns were showcased currently only in **Wastelands of Baedoor** mod, in two variants being
regular shotgun and its sawed off variant, dealing more damage in exchange for bigger durability
penalty.  
In reality though, various regular variants exist, and it's hard to tell which ayer shotgun could be
considered a blueprint for all others.
