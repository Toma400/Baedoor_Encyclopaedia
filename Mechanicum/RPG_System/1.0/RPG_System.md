# 🎲 RPG System 1.0

Baedoor RPG System was designed as inherent aspect of all RPG games taking place in Baedoor universe.  
Its origins date back to version 1.0, which is the only one that has no rules written, as its main premise
was to avoid rules whatsoever.  
While statistics existed (and were pretty big part), the whole system resided in head of game master
(who was also a player) and could be bent to the story - the point of the game was to tell a history
of player, oftentimes more in a form of immersive simulator than a true roleplaying experience.

That focus on immersivity and sim aspects will be seen in every iteration and the whole approach
towards how the worldbuilding is made as a whole, and how it relates to things using it.

Main points of version 1.0:
- Very loose rules, focus is on immersivity and sim elements, "living day-by-day" in universe
