# 🎴 Upcoming changes for 4th Edition

**4th Edition of Baedoor RPG System** is just around the corner. Neccessity of it is seen for me right after setting 3rd one in stone: there are a lot of missing aspects of lore which didn't get through.

While 3rd Edition will be maintained for a while for `The Isle of Ansur` game, upcoming games will stick to 4th one, to solidify connection to lore.

## Planned features
- Introduction of magic paths, which are different from common magic from 3rd edition; 
both ways stay alongside though, being just alternatives (path magic is stronger, 
but closes way to another forms of path magic instead).
- Mores and scrolls usage gets more sophisticated by addition of new `scroll writing` and `mores casting`
skills - so now you will not longer be able to use `spellcasting` for all types of magic, but will need
to decide whether to invest in one or another.
- Huge extension of races, both new ones, as well as splitting some into subraces.
   - Splitting ghlodds into subraces of Seiteh and reconstructing their statistics accordingly
   - Adding zhi race
- Big addition of new classes, extending variety for non-fighting choices. Balancing already existing ones and possibly changing bilance to +2 to make classes more valuable during character creation.
   - Path Mage - mage class which has no special bonus, but instead it gives entry 
                 to one specific magic path immediately
   - Barbarian - invests strongly in toughness and handfight, has new perk Barbarian 
                 (decreased pain and increased damage if low health), has weakness on guns 
                  and magic handling
   - Traveller - class slightly supporting traveller skills, as well as giving you special 
                 prices in inns and taverns (mostly for sleepovers)
- During character creation, new option to determine religious affiliation is added (which grants
  additional bonuses, is used later in lore gameplay and for marriage)
- Addition of new skills and activities (first edition parity):
   - Activities:
     - Fishing
- Fixing some inconsistencies within perks and skills:
   - Removal of `Vehicles Driving` and `Animal Training` perks - adding `Transportation` and 
     `Animal Taming` skills instead, where `Transportation` is separated into different subskills
     on different vehicles, as well as horse riding and boat driving
   - `Toxin Immunity` is split into perk (becoming boolean value) and skill `Toxin Absorption`. Perk
     is equal to 100% toxin immunity (as the name says) and skill grants you bonuses against toxins
     (such as shorter times, less punishment and rarer chances of getting one)
- Added temperature for locations as one of survival factors. Regarding this aspect, there are several
  features added:
   - Illnesses gained through getting your body on specific temperature for too long, drinking uncooked
     water and so on
   - More survival actions possible in general, such as creating campfires and more immersive cooking
     (mostly related to cRPG)
- Hunger and thirst are no longer optional values, adding to temperature importance
- Added house-related details, such as:
   - Use of customised furnishing (cRPG only aspect)
   - Locations can be now "wilderness", "city" and "suburbs" which applies to different factors:
     - You cannot create new home on "city" nor "suburbs" areas, you need to buy already existing
       or buy the area from city/owner
     - You cannot destroy environment on neither "city" nor "suburbs" as well to gather materials
