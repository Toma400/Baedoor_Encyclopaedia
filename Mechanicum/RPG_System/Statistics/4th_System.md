# 🎴 Upcoming changes for 4th Edition

**4th Edition of Baedoor RPG System** is just around the corner. Neccessity of it is seen for me right after setting 3rd one in stone: there are a lot of missing aspects of lore which didn't get through.

While 3rd Edition will be maintained for a while for `The Isle of Ansur` game, upcoming games will stick to 4th one, to solidify connection to lore.

## Planned features
- Introduction of magic paths, which are different from common magic from 3rd edition; 
both ways stay alongside though, being just alternatives (path magic is stronger, 
but closes way to another forms of path magic instead).
- Huge extension of races, both new ones, as well as splitting some into subraces.
   - Splitting ghlodds into subraces of Seiteh and reconstructing their statistics accordingly
   - Adding zhi race
- Big addition of new classes, extending variety for non-fighting choices. Balancing already existing ones and possibly changing bilance to +2 to make classes more valuable during character creation.
   - Path Mage - mage class which has no special bonus, but instead it gives entry 
                 to one specific magic path immediately
   - Barbarian - invests strongly in toughness and handfight, has new perk Barbarian 
                 (decreased pain and increased damage if low health), has weakness on guns 
                  and magic handling
   - Traveller - class slightly supporting traveller skills, as well as giving you special 
                 prices in inns and taverns (mostly for sleepovers)
- Addition of new skills and activities (first edition parity).
