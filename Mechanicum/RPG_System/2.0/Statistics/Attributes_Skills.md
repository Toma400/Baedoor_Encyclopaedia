# ♟️ Secondary modifiers

Attributes are passive traits giving background to your basic modifiers. 
They can also rarily serve as bonus or modifier for some of your actions.

Skills, in the other hand, are results of your own work - and, samely as in life, the more you train, the better outcome you get.

---
**ATTRIBUTES**  
By default, all attributes are at level 8.

- STR (Strength)
- AGI (Agility)
- END (Endurance)
- INT (Intelligence)
- CHA (Charisma)

**SKILLS**
- Swords¹
- Bows¹
- Firearms¹
- Spellcasting
- Trade¹
- Connection²
- Repair
- Healing
- Lockpicking
- Sneaking
- Smithing
- Herbalism
- Vehicle Driving
- Traps Spotting
- Survival

**PERKS**  
Not expandable, once-gatherable (with race, class or by learning).  
They were not used in the game and it is unclear whether they appeared in 2.0 or 3.0 exactly.

- Trader Sight
- Vehicles Driving
- Assassin's Hit
- Toxin Immunity
- Stheyr
- Friend of Animals
- Vampirism
- Body Harvesting

---

¹ - one point guaranteed by default  
² - meant for characters having positive connection alignment, but allowed to be changed freely
