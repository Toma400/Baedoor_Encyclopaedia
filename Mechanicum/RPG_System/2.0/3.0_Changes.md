# 🎴 3.0 Changes
Baedoor RPG System 3.0 introduces several changes, such as:

- General
  - Deprecation of morality index - it may come back somewhere in the future, but its existence
    served no purpose and had no real use in Between Shadows and Light anyway
  - Detoxication (DTX) is deprecated in favour of `toxin absorption` as part of perk rewrite
  - Hunger and Thirst are no longer optional
- Skills
  - Heavy expansion of available skills, either by separating old ones or adding new
  - Skills are less "learnable" as achievement, and more as a matter of regular work
    (similarly to TES series)
- Perks
  - They are no longer in fluid boolean/integer state, making it hard to put into real RPG system
    - Boolean-based perks are called **perks**, as previously
    - Integer-based perks are now called **abilities**
      (not to be confused with **attributes** such as strength)
