# 🎴 3.0 Changes
Baedoor RPG System 3.0 introduces several changes, such as:

- General
  - Deprecation of morality index - it may come back somewhere in the future, but its existence
    served no purpose and had no real use in Between Shadows and Light anyway
  - Detoxication (DTX) is deprecated in favour of `toxin absorption` as part of perk rewrite
  - Hunger and Thirst are no longer optional
- Character creation
  - Balanced build is changed to empower races and classes
    - Race has now ... to attributes and ... to skills
    - Class now has ... to skills
    - Manually picked skill now raises +2, instead of +1 as previously
  - Class change
    - Classes now put additional experience gain on specific skills, 
      making it more useful on long-term play
      (they no longer serve as only starting bonus points provider)
- Skills
  - Heavy expansion of available skills, either by separating old ones or adding new
    - First edition parity is prioritised, bringing back many activities/skills
  - Skills are less "learnable" as achievement, and more as a matter of regular work
    (similarly to TES series)
- Fixing some inconsistencies within perks and skills:
   - Removal of `Vehicles Driving` and `Animal Training` perks - adding `Transportation` category and 
     `Animal Taming` skills instead, where `Transportation` is separated into different subskills
     on different vehicles, like horse riding and boat driving
   - `Toxin Immunity` is split into perk (becoming boolean value) and skill `Toxin Absorption`. Perk
     is equal to 100% toxin immunity (as the name says) and skill grants you bonuses against toxins
     (such as shorter times, less punishment and rarer chances of getting one)
- Perks
  - They are no longer in fluid boolean/integer state, making it hard to put into real RPG system
    - Boolean-based perks are called **perks**, as previously
    - Integer-based perks are now called **abilities**
      (not to be confused with **attributes** such as strength).
      Unlike skills they are not "grind-to-earn", but more abstract to gain
  - Races that have no penalty from TECH/MAGCK or VOID/CONN powers now have those described by
    perks instead of additional feature, for simplicity reasons
