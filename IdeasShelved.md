# Ideas Shelved
There are various ideas that have nice worldbuilding potential, but they do not have
place in currently worked areas of the world. Instead of putting them artificially and trying
to fit in, I think it'd be best to just enlist them and come back to them later.

### Tears' glyph
Five/seven straight vertical lines that lengthen towards the centre.

Initially used as iconography for my Drżenie project, I found it as interesting visual
to be used for particular faith. It does also feel a little referential for Tamriel Rebuilt's
Ordinators in Mourning which character would fit really nicely.

### Warrior Dancers
Taken out of Lai-Kine doc because, while somewhat fitting to pahtri/saphtri, I'm not entirely
sure if it fits them. It also adds yet another interesting thing to their culture, which by
itself is not an issue, but makes me more hesitant not being sure on the vibe.

Description from the doc:
```
There's a class of warriors among Lai-kine, The Joyous and saphtris that derive from the 
ecstatic nature of eastern tris culture. Warrior dancers are known for their incredible 
agility and working upon the body first and foremost. Their appearance on the field resembles 
one of dancing monks, whirling and stepping between enemies smoothly.

Warrior dancers usually value light armour and weapons, so that they can put the whole focus 
on moving.
```
Comment from the doc:
```
Idea of warrior dancers could be moved to another civ; it fits tris and imo works within 
this setting, but I'm worrying they get too much goodness :P

Ofc joking a little, from all things they only got shadow painters so far, and dancers are 
kinda idea of tris as a whole (at least w/o voi heretics) so it works more generally.. 
and why would we limit ourselves so much anyway

But just putting it up here - can be moved if there's culture equally fitting but more 
imperialistic, my main thought is that pahtri are not particularly militaristic 
(which is why extending this to saphtri was logical both from religious POV 
(as antic from the past cultivated) and being more inline with their combat focus)
```
