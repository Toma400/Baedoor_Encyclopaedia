# ⛩️ Higher District
**Higher District** is district of [At'har](../At'har.md), built on the rock cliff separating
western and eastern parts of the city. Roads leading between them cross the district, making it
a bridge between allowing to traverse up and down depending on need.
The name of the district is not related to wealth by any means, and is not seen as particularly
desirable destination by aristocratic ghlodds.
