# 🌆 At'har

---
<span style="color:#107C23">**cities**</span> [✦](/Loreum/Tags.md#-cities)  

---
**At'har** is big ghloddish city found on eastern side of [Seiteh](../../Seiteh.md), separated from
[Fa'iteh](../Lands/Fa'iteh.md) by [Tar Tahraes mountains](../Lands/Tar_Tahraes.md).  
It is considered as one of the biggest trade centers of the continent, as it was one of the few
settlements that prospered without interference into any bigger conflicts. It was also somewhat
frequent neutral ground to mediate between two sides.
