**Ghenash'r** (ghl. _ghe nash'r_, "home of the soul") is a city found inside [Ghe'ar Ethr cave](../Lands/Ghe'ar_Ethr.md),
spreading inside internal plateau along the river going across.

### 🪔 HISTORY
Ghenash'r was one of the more prosperous ghloddish cities before the migration to surface world,
and even after it remains one of the most significant settlements due to its history and culture,
as well as mystics pilgrimages.

### 🎨 ART
Art of Ghenash'r is known in entire [Seiteh](../../Seiteh.md) due to its characteristic touch
emboldening mystical inspirations through ornamental design and lime dyeing. Being influenced
by Say'ye'ys thought, such form of artistry was meant to bring them prosperity and protection
of dragon's wisdom over the city.

After migration, Ghenash'r art became widely known, expanding [Say'ye'ys cult](/Loreum/Topics/Religions/Say'ye'ys_Cult.md)
more broadly.

### 🎉 HOLIDAYS
Before the Isheism, Ghenash'r celebrated its own holidays that existed only within its own
community and faith.
- Spark Festival - happening the night in between tenth and eleventh of August, was a moment of
                   reminisce of the departed, as well as those still living; each ghlodd had
                   obligation to "light the fading other" with their own spark (hence the name).
                   Spark in this sense meant memory, but also in broader sense our experience,
                   teachings and strength we have to offer.
