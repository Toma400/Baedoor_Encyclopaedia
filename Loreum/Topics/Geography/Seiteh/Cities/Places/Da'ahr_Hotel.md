# 🖼️ Da'ahr Hotel
Da'ahr Hotel is sizeable hotel built on vertical hill of the [Higher District](../Districts/At'har_Higher_District.md),
in the center of [At'har](../At'har.md). Its arrangement doesn't stray away from regular hotel, 
offering moderate prices and amenities, frequently accommodating various wanderers of 
varied wealth.  
The hotel also includes library room, allowing visitors to read most classics of ghloddish and
international literature.
