# 🏕️ Gheanah
**Gheanah** (ghl. _A'nah Ghe'e'ana_, literally "Pit of the Great Spirit"), known also less formally
as "Gorge of Death", is the main passage between valley made by [Tar Tahraes](Tar_Tahraes.md) and
northern Seiteh.

Gorge of Death has its name coined by the infamous terrain and mysterious events following those
who wandered in. With characteristic rock formations narrowing towards the center, that central
throat ended up being perfect location for ambushes and military defences.  
On the other hand, natural winds going through the gorge and its rocks make sound that feels
ethereal, unsettling, building tension through uneven routes and feeling as if coming from 
multiple places at once. That also makes Gheanah a place considered to be lived by spirits.
The more superstitious wanderers might fear if crossing the gorge won't anger them.
