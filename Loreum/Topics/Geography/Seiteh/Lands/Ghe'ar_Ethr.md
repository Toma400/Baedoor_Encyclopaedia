# 🏕️ Ghe'ar Ethr
**Ghe'ar Ethr** is one of the biggest caves found on [Seiteh](../../Seiteh.md) continent. Being
located in [E'anatere' mountain](E'anatare'.md), it is known for holding ancient ghloddish city
[Ghenash'r](../Cities/Ghenash'r.md) and being a lair of [Say'ye'ys](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Say'ye'ys.md).

### Shape
Ghe'ar Ethr is enormous cavern with stalagnate-like rocky supports of unusual thickness. Through
a recess found inside two underground rivers are going through, one of which sections the cave
in half. The second one is relatively tiny and continues its flow through another recess to the
outside of E'anatare' mountain.
