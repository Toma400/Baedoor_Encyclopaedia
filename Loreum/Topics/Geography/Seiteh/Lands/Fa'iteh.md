# 🏕️ Fa'iteh Desert
**Fa'iteh** is the most known and biggest desert of the universe, covering heavy chunk of dry
areas of [Seiteh continent](../../Seiteh.md).

It's known as one of the **Fa deserts**, although the danger of Fa'iteh lies mostly on its 
vastness and less so on extreme environments. There are rare big cave entrances found there, making
it possible to survive traversing entire length by foot, if with enough knowledge.
