# 🏕️ Tar Tahraes
**Tar Tahraes** (ghl. _Ta'r Ta'hra'oe's_, literally "Stone Close To Sky", poetically "Stone 
Touching Sky") is a mountain range with crescent shape, creating a divider between eastern
coasts and [Fa'iteh](Fa'iteh.md).  
Tar Tahraes is known for its defensive potential, which is the reason why to this day a lot of 
fort and watchtower ruins can be seen dotting its landscapes.
