# 🌄 Mes Inpea

<img alt="Mes Inpea map" width="562" height="415" src="/Assets/Maps/mes_inpea.png">

Mes Inpea is the largest island on Old Continent, being also the most intriguing climate-wise.  
While covered in huge part in deserty wastelands and rocky cliffs, it also has lush green habitat
at opposite part of the island. Additionally, Mes Inpea even include some cold deserts, however
very different from ice ones known from lands above it.
