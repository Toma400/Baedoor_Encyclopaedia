# 🖼️ Flower Street
Flower Street (jit. *Enfrvein*) is representative street of [Menerun](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Jitado/Cities/Menerun.md)
city, located in **Evrun** district. It is known for scenical flower patterns, creating gardens around
the street. It is main symbol of district's prosperity.
