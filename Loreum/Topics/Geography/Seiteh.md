# 🌄 Seiteh

**Seiteh** (also ghl. *Seyta'h*, sometimes *Seyteygh*, from ehr. *Ct'eh*) is one of the main
continents in Baedoor universe, being a second largest one only after **Moon Continent**. It is
inhabited primarily by [ghlodds](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/Ghlodds/[Culture].md).

The name is taken from ehric description, as in Old Dialect it meant "entire world".

### Landscapes
For such a vast land, the territory of Seiteh holds the biggest desert area of the universe,
covering huge swathe of land in between somewhat fertile northern coast and tropical southern
jungles. The deserts are crossed by multiple mountain ranges, giving the way for many places that
are isolated. Ghlodds for centuries built many fortifications in various seemingly-forgotten
areas, creating this sort of guerilla-like defences in case of such need. These over years became
home for bandits, cults or anything else that had any interest in inhabiting such buildings.

A huge part of Seiteh environment is hidden in underground caves. They sometimes carve their
way out to the surface with huge entrances, but otherwise much more often need to have those
passages dug and constructed artificially. A lot of bigger cities have their own elevators allowing
traversing up and down for anyone willing to explain their reasoning.

Underground caves are vast and with their own, humid and colder environment. Water condenses in
small lakes, and the unique flora grows there, emitting dim colourful light and being food source
for those who live underground. Ghlodds living under the surface however are not as common as in
the times of Pre Era, and these communities usually live somewhat isolated from what is happening
on the surface.
