**Kacari** is the biggest island satelliting [Kaer](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer.md)
that also holds position of being a sanctuary of [erds](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/Erds/[Culture].md).

Covered heavily by dense forests, it holds one of two capitals of erdic state, the city of Roda.  
Other than that, its notable settlements are trading town of Kananea and fishing village located
on southern coast, Anoan, deriving name from past town of Anoltan built further east.  
In addition, Kacari hosts the biggest temple of erdic faith, and major research institution,
University of Illusion.

### CITIES
- Roda

### SMALLER SETTLEMENTS
- Kananea
- Anoan
- Anoltan `ruined`

### PLACES
- [University of Illusion](Places/University_of_Illusion.md)

### LANDSCAPES
- Mesaul Mountains `west range on Kacari`
- Lemeris Mountains `east range on Kacari`
- Dair River `the river going south of Temple, out of Mesaul`
- Oin Forest `forest south of Roda`
