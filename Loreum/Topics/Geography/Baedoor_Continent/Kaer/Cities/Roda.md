# 🌆 Roda

---
<span style="color:#107C23">**cities**</span> [✦](/Loreum/Tags.md#-cities)  

---
**Roda** is the biggest city of southern islands of [Baedoor Continent](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent.md),
located on the bay of [Kacari island](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer/Kacari.md).  
It is considered the most important and sacred city of [erdic culture](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/Erds/[Culture].md),
being its spiritual and partially administrative capital. It is not only an important place
for religious pilgrimages, but also a bustling trade and artistic center.

### Banner
Roda's banner feature the depiction of lush garden, symbolising the concept by which the place
is considered a sacred sanctuary of erdic culture. The floral motifs have a bit of spiral
nature, however not being too obvious to usual viewer, denoting spiritual meaning of the
depiction.  
The banner has two eyes positioned in between each other, one closed and one opened - the 
motif makes it look like they have some internal connection or flow between each other, as
is the understanding of future and the past represented by those symbols.
