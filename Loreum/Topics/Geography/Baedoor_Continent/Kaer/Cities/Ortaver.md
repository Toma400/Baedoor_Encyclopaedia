**Ortaver** (also called *Detemardir* under erdic rule in PE) is kaerian-ruled city south of
Mavsvi river, a buzzling hub built on mining, trade and artisan craft.  
Built in Pre Era by erds, it is known to be one of the most restless places on Kaer.
It experienced multiple sieges damaging it, at earliest between erds and kehts, through 
wars of kaerians and erds, to finally be a focal point of baedoorians invasion in OE.

Ortaver was rebuilt multiple times, meaning besides few buildings and some parts of erdic wall
still standing to this day, most houses and fortifications are relatively recent.  
The city however expanded widely, including rural areas outside its city walls.

Currently, the city struggles with rising tensions with erdic neighbours, proclaiming the city
is historically theirs and that the treaty was written in favour of kaerians who excavate
natural resources. While the demand to bring city back were stated for centuries now, the
recent kaerian expansionism seems to push that into risky direction that Kaer cultures tried
to avoid for all this time.
