# 🌾 University of Illusion

---
**University of Illusion** is the oldest academic and research institution founded by [erds](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/Erds/[Culture].md).  
Built on [Kacari](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer/Kacari.md), it is a destination
for many adepts, be it interested in broad variety of academic topics studied there, or to learn
nuances of ancient erdic magic.  
The university is heavily backed financially by the Roda governor, with fair share of funds
brought also from Kaer.

The name of the university is taken from erdic reading on word "illusion" that does mean to them
a reality in itself, with its oneiric and impenetrable nature.
