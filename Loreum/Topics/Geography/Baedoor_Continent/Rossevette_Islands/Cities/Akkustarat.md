**Akkustarat** is ancient [latoka](../../../../Cultures/Latoka/[Culture].md) city and a capital of
**Nan Ammarat realm**. Sometimes referred as "the city of glass", it's known for its striking
architecture that utilises colour and glass giving impression of blissful light descending into
the city from afar.
