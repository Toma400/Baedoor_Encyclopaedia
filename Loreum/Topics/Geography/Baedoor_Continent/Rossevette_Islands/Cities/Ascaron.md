**Ascaron** (sometimes also referred by latoka as *Assaron*) is a city and a capital of **Ascaron Realm**.
Holding its influence over its part of western coast and **Emerald Hills**, it relies heavily 
on mining various resources and fishing, with some lesser takings coming out of trade held 
in **Tarsis** and distributed in Ascaron grand market.

The city itself is built upon centuries of slow expansion.

[The city is one of the most favourable to baedoorians and is (together with Tarsis) one of
their frequent destinations because of that (obviously Nid-Iliot is another big port)]

### History
Ascaron was established around ruins of old settlement called **Leveiihat**. [Group of latoka
families of Tarsis-Abar culture ventured there to search for possibility to rebuild the glory
of previous place.] # note that it wasn't meant to be glorious at first

...

After [peace treaty](/Loreum/Topics/History/List/Varteford_Peace_Treaty.md), Ascaron remained
as the only so faithful realm to baedoorians, keeping the name and buildings without stripping
them to local architecture. This is specifically seen in Ascaron's walls being heavily baedoorian, 
with only one segment rebuilt after it fell into disrepair.

+ Ascaron holds the biggest baedoorian bank's building?
