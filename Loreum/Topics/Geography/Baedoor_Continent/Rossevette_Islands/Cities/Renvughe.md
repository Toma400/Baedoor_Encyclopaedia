# 🌆 Renvughe

---
<span style="color:#107C23">**cities**</span> [✦](/Loreum/Tags.md#-cities)  

---
**Renvughe** is a hamlet found in [Shimo Valley](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Rossevette_Islands/Regions/Shimo_Valley.md),
founded by baedoorians during **Baedoor Empire** occupation. Initially just a very tiny outpost
set to oversee the valley, with poor living conditions and bad reputation, it became much more 
habitable after handing off to **Likkrot-Nanat realm** as a result of Empire's retreat due 
to [peace treaty](/Loreum/Topics/History/List/Varteford_Peace_Treaty.md).
