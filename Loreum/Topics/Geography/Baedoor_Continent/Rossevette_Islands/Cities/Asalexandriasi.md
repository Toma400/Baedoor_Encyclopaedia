# 🌆 Asalexandriasi

---
<span style="color:#107C23">**cities**</span> [✦](/Loreum/Tags.md#-cities)  

---
**Asalexandriasi** is an administrative and military outpost, built on top of defunct mining village.
It hosts the minimal amount of housing needed for nearby mine to operate, for profit logging
and providing transport of resources down the river.
