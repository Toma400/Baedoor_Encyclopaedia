# 🌆 Karos

---
<span style="color:#107C23">**cities**</span> [✦](/Loreum/Tags.md#-cities)  

---
**Karos** is an independent city-state built on **Karos** island. Originally built by baedoorians
during their occupation, it became a sovereign state after **Varteford's Peace Treaty**.

Karos is fairly unique port, as it shows all influences building its society. Its citizens count
pahtris, latokas, voitri and sporadically even goblins for which Karos is the only settlement
they can feel welcomed to migrate to.  
The architecture also resembles the past, sliced between old city in baedoorian architecture,
through outskirt districts having its unique buildings being a mix of pahtric and latoka styles.
