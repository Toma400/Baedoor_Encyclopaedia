# 🏰 Emerald Hills
Emerald Hills is the tiny pocket of hilly lowlands located on northernwest coast of [Rossevette](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Rossevette_Islands.md),
encompassing current extent of **Ascaron Realm** rule.  
Built from somewhat conflicting terrain features, Emerald Hills' geography dictates how it was
settled - the region is dominated by meadows and lowlands, along which most latoka built their
houses and farms.  
On the other hand, there are also steeper, hilly parts that are much harder to traverse, keeping
such places more desolate. They are occupied mostly by outcasts or become rare destination
of mining expeditions.

Despite areas that come as more challenging, the region is pretty lush and abundant in fertile
areas to be cultivated, making it something of a breadbasket of the wider community to which
food produced by Ascaron farmers is exported. From that comes the name - emphasizing on the 
rich character of the area, not the existence of emeralds themselves.
