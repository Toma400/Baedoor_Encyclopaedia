# 🏰 Shimo Valley

**Shimo Valley** is a name for a region surrounded by two nearby mountainous ranges. The region
is fertile and fairly friendly to live in. Its lands are dotted by farms and not that uncommon
latoka tombs - as the area is sometimes called "a land of tombs" due to their more frequent
appearance.
