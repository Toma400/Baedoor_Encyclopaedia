# 🌆 Dvickhet

---
<span style="color:#107C23">**cities**</span> [✦](/Loreum/Tags.md#-cities)  

---
**Dvickhet** is town located near Baedoor and Isuga, with origins dating back to Pre Era, as it is one
of those settlements built by nomathians.

It is relatively poor, yet big town, known for its numerous diasporas and biggest nomathian minority in 
southern and western side of this country. It is considered as ruled by [Warthog family](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/Families/Warthog.md), 
however over the course of New Era, **Maen family** became the second leading power.

### History

Conquered in early Old Era, Dvickhet initially experienced efflux of population to neighbouring cities 
with better prospects. With town's size putting strain on maintaining it for ruling families - the 
nomathian Warthog and Thargav, and baedoorian family Maen - the city became poorer.  
Over the middle years of Old Era, Warthog family put a lot of thought into bringing the town to its
previous stability. Their rulers connected a lot with families from Isuga, Ontero and Baedoor, while
in the same time Warthogs passed the law that brought weapon manufacturers to the city.
With [Ettua family](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/Families/Ettua.md) helping on coordinating metal 
industry with newly made factories, it helped immensely to bring new people to the town.

Next centuries resulted in slow rise of Dvickhet, getting hands in metal industry and expanding to 
economy branches related, which were underrepresented by nearby cities. This also helped the Warthog 
family to get back money invested previously and also become important power in the area.  
Over the course of conquests, Dvickhet provided a lot of support to various invasions, enabling
Empire's army to have good amount of needed equipment.

Warthog's ruling was very profitable to Dvickhet, and the family tried its best to utilise the power 
to enrich the town. In a way, this was successful attempt.  
That said, Warthog's fall in IXth century brought Dvickhet into another round of decay - albeit this 
time, the process was much slower, as the town had a lot of resources to burn through.

In early New Era however, the effect of Warthog losing their grip and power was evident - the town
couldn't find itself sustainable on the long run, at least in the same way it was before.
Some districts started to visibly get poorer, as they couldn't afford maintenance and the town 
state had other costs to cover.  
This is also the time when Maen, backed by baedoorian families from other cities, started to matter,
serving as a puppet to control Warthog.
