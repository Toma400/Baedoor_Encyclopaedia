# 🌆 Morpat

---
<span style="color:#107C23">**cities**</span> [✦](/Loreum/Tags.md#-cities)  

---
**Morpat** is a small city of nomathian origins, founded as one of earlier settlements in
this area. While rich and important in the old days, as a main passage town to the Vershaton,
it was outpaced by quickly developing [Alkaesar](Alkaesar.md). Raising the latter to the main city of
Baedoor Empire influence buried Morpat's chances to thrive forever, rerouting the roads in favour of
new trade centre.
