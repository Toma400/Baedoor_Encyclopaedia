**Thanbyan** is one of the oldest settlements built on [Baedoor Island](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor.md),
known for its importance in mineral extraction and history.

### History
Initially it served as a home for group of priests residing in isolated place, at
that time also being a sort of oasis with many water sources - allowing the small monastery
town to build first houses and be relatively self-sufficient.

With desertation slowly approaching Thanbyan and nomathians expanding their areas over Vershaton,
citizens of the city started increasing their contacts with nearby towns, helping each other. This
put the end to Thanbyan seen as isolated monastery town and slowly introduced it into its new
industrial era.  
Over the next centuries, Thanbyan expanded heavily and became an industrial town, pivoting towards
excavation of minerals and ores that environs of Vershaton vulcano was rich of.

During jitadean invasion, Thanbyan was one of the few Vershaton settlements that were hard to
conquer and so it only made the town grow in importance for nomathians. Ultimately, it gave in, with
decent part of the place being destroyed. As invaders saw the potential in the town, they helped
with its rebuilding, taking the reign over its resources. Later in this era, the railroad line
between Arindun and Thanbyan was built, helping with transport of valuable resources outside.

At the end of Old Era, enormous eruption of Vershaton vulcano laid the city to waste. Thanbyan
was covered with the lava and ash, making the place inaccessible.  
In next decades, several baedoorian families (including also nomathian descendants) initiated
restoration project that was meant to rebuild the city few miles away from where ruins of old
Thanbyan existed. The initiative was meant to restore its mining potential, with nomathian's calls
to rebuild sacred areas being ignored.
