# 🏕️ Amber Forest

|        ![](/Assets/Landscapes/Regions/amber_forest_bas.png)        |
|:------------------------------------------------------------------:|
| Amber Forest concept art made for Tamriel Rebuilt by be-a-stranger |

**Amber Forest** is region found on western coast of [Baedoor Island](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor.md),
covered by mountains from eastern side. It is populated semi-densely with trees of extensive roots,
covered with orange foliage year-round. Some trees yield amber, as a remnant of times when Agoi
civilisation landed on the area and started terraforming it for their use.

### Trivia
Amber Forest was region originally meant to be found in [Tamriel Rebuilt mod](https://www.tamriel-rebuilt.org/).
Upon discussions however it was discarded, meaning all the beautiful concepts no longer serve any
use.  
As a huge fan of the region's conceptualisation, I was wondering if I wouldn't be able to use it
in some way in Baedoor universe. The choice of western baedoorian coast felt very natural - not
only it originally was meant to have orangey-yellow colour pallette in its new iteration, but it
also fits very well with Agoi nearby.  
The subtle differences from CA that Baedoor's AF would have is primarily lesser density of the
flora, with also a bit more variety for trees.
