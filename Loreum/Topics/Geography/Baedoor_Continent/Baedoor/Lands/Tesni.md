# 🏕️ Tesni
Tesni is an island located in [Baedoor Island](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor.md),
south of [Old Baedoor](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Baedoor.md).
The Tesni bridge connects it to the City itself, which is frequented by noble carriage.  

Tesni, also known as the Estate Isle, is home to the villa town of Quinaet and the faux-rural ville
of Kennusa.  
Quinaet is a clean Baedoor-style resort town on the island's northern slopes 
comprising intersecting lanes of villas and gardens, and is inhabited seasonally by many of 
Baedoor's upper class who rent out properties here to members of the bourgeois. 
Thus, the town contains few services due to lacking permanent population, however a spa and 
general store as well as some other necessities can be found at the town's two ends.  
On the end of Quinaet furthest from the bridge entrance is a path leading down across the island
to the faux rural village of Kennusa, a village meant to imitate the peaceful and quaint aesthetics 
of rural Baedoor for its holidaying guests (despite lacking real hardships and thus the authentic
flavour of a rural lifestyle). Kennusa is also home to a small port due to being located on the
island's only beach.  
Outside of the two "settlements" Tesni's landscape and flora are notably tamed and orderly for
the purposes of providing pleasant vistas and strolls for the island's residents. 
The island's coast is all low cliffs with the exception of Kennusa as it lacks any natural 
beaches; the beach at Kennusa is manmade.
