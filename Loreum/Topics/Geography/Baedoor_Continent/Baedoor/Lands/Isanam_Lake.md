# 🏕️ Isanam Lake
**Isanam** is lake formed deep in [Baedoor Island](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor.md),
holding waters from mountain range of the north and being a stopgap for waters that flow down
to the sea.  
Isanam holds three islands, with the biggest one being occupied by city of [Isuga](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Isuga.md).
