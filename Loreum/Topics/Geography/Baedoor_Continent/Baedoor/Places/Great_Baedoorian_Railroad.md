# 🌾 Great Baedoorian Railroad
First and the longest railroad line. Built by **Baedoor Railroad Enterprise** in 712 OE and became
the most popular way to travel on longer distances, known for its speed and safety.

### History
First line was built in parts, starting from 712 OE with Baedoor-Isuga connection and expanding
consecutively as the train initiative deemed successful, with additional funds being put into
the project.  
Around 770 OE, the second line was projected and four years later, the slow and similarly
modular project of creating the railroad was developed. The project was finished at the beginnings
of eight century, and in 820 OE, the Arindun-Thanbyan minor line was built to help with Thanbyan's
booming industry that needed to rely on slow wagon transport before that.

In similar time, Baedoor Railroad Enterprise introduced new, reinforced trains that were meant
to solve the issue of trains being frequently targeted by bandits and goblin clans. Later in this
era, some trains were also armed with additional firepower when mounted guns were invented.

Third line was projected right after second one was built, being lobbied by parties across many
areas that saw this as opportunity to help gathering resources even easier, without relying
on long and dangerous Vershaton line.  
As expected, Skirran, Alkaesar and Nahsig families tried to interrupt the project as this yielded
their line much less lucrative, but that opposing force was marginal compared to lobbying force
for even further expansion. In 824 OE, third line was started and its construction was finished
four years later, connecting Pervesi with Dvickhet and offloading slightly marine transport
from the former.
