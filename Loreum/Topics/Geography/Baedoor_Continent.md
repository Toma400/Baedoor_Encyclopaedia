Baedoor Continent is neatly located in between [Mes Inpea](Old_Continent/Mes_Inpea.md) and
[Moon Continent](Moon_Continent.md), and is basically a collection of islands of huge size.

It had major role in events of Old Era, becoming heart of its industrial revolution.  
Aside of it, it saw rise of Baedoor Empire, from which its name come from.
