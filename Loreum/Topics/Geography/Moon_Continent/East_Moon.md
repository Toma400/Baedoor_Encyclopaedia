# 🌄 East Moon
East Moon is part of **Moon Continent**, on the east side of **the Scarp**.

### GEOGRAPHY

<img alt="East Moon Sketch" height="520" src="/Assets/Maps/east_moon.png" width="390"/>

``Sketch of East Moon, with incorrectly shown Agoi Archipelago which should be positioned way higher``

East Moon is only part of bigger continent, but it has its own distinct character, as it was torn
from the rest in unnoted, ancient times. The reason for it is unknown, but some people believe this
could be a result of some battle, whether between powerful mages or even gods.
