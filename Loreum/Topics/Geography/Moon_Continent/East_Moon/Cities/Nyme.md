# 🌆 Nyme

<img alt="Nyme" src="/Assets/ansur_nyme.jpg" width="390" height="520" />

**Nyme** is little village located on western coast of [**Ansur Isle**](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon/Ansur.md), 
built to overlook nearby iron mine. Initially it only served as home for administrative building,
warehouse and little harbour with ship transporting the resources up to Evros.

Over time, the village expanded with new buildings around, built by vineyard owners and farmers, as
well as just people willing to settle.  
Eventually, Nyme became a way for vine traders to avoid taxes imposed in Evros, undermining some of
its sources of income. It was never enough of a bother though to justify governor taking initiative.
