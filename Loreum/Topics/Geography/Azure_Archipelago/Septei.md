**Septei** is one of [Azure Archipelago](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Azure_Archipelago.md) islands located
in between [Moon](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent.md) and [Seiteh](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Seiteh.md)
continents. It is held by local tri settlers who discovered it in early Pre Era and since then 
became independent state to their origins, building their own cultural offshoot.  
Because of the vicinity to **Oe'os**, Septei was frequent target of aggression from ghloddish 
forces, which kept their dependency to mainland tris for many centuries (but also what led to 
septeians becoming independent, as a neutral state).

Septei architecture is commonly built from red wood with soaring, greenish roofs, with a lot of structures
to work around elevation and navigation. Septei towns are often deeply buried in forests, with trees
being part of their identity, providing shelter from rain and sun. Town streets are therefore characterised
by peaceful dimness and being lighted by sunrays getting through tree leaves, and lanterns during
the night.
