**Vartee** (ghl. _Ua'rte'thord'oe'_, shortened also to _Ua'rte'oe'_) is a microscopic satellite
island to **Oe'os**. It consists of small marshy field and a little mountainous area rising at the
back of it.  
Since late Old Era, it holds small abandoned village and manor. Some rumours say as if pirates
used it as a temporary home, but this was never proven by Oe'os Navy.

### History
For most of its history, the island was uninhabited, but ghlodds formally claimed their ownership
during settling of Oe'os. Being so small and of no value, there was no reason for them to build
anything.

In 971 OE however, Vartee was given to [Tom Varteford](../../Characters/List/Varteford_Tom.md) as an act
of gratitude. It was then named as "Varteford's Island" in ghloddish, but shortened to simply
Vartee by baedoorians.  
Tom build a small house and treated this place as a vacation residence. It later expanded into
entire manor and plantation, and with hired baedoorian workers, Tom managed the building of the
village port serving island as supply delivery. With that, he invited ghlodds to come infrequently
and provide services in time of his visit.

That however fell with death of Tom, as Varteford family wasn't interested in upholding such
faraway property, and ghlodds weren't particularly interested in regaining ownership over it.
With time, Vartee's village fell into disrepair. Ever since, the island is only occasionally
visited by random ships trying to survive storms.
