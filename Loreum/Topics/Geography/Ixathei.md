# 🌄 Ixathei

**Ixathei** is a relatively small continent found south of [Baedoor Continent](Baedoor_Continent.md),
composed of collection of islands, alongside the biggest one that dominates the archipelago.  
Ixathei is primarily covered in jungle and tropical regions, and is home to many diverse creature
races living there for millennia, developing their unique cultures and relationships.
