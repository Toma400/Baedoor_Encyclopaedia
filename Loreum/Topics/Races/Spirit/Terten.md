# 💮 Tertens

**Tertens** are one of the most frequently met and varied undead races, yet their character is strictly
intertwined with them being also spirits. There are countless variations of tertens roaming the
Baedoor universe, and samely each creature can get wildly different in any form it represents.

Terten can be considered both as a race and creature, since their behaviour and character has shared
traits of both - they can have a culture, interact with others and be part of civilised world; yet,
they often separate themselves from the world to lesser or bigger degree, and they can be very
unpredictable or individualistic.

Their origins and how they appear in the world are still a mystery. Two most common theories are that
tertens are either souls that couldn't enter Lands of the Dead, or souls from another universe that
got lost.  
Their appearance differ wildly depending on ways the soul creates its body, as it has infinite ways
to do so. Usually souls inhabit dead bodies, thus becoming a form that is indistinguishable from mere
undead to most people. Some souls shape their body by molding materials instead, creating form that is
almost an individualistic self-expression.  

The difference between tertens and other undeads is that the former usually have purpose to exist.  
Terten either tries to fulfill that reason, or tries to live in this new form, as biological needs
no longer bond them to the physical world limitations.
