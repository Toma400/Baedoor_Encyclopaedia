# 💮 Flesh Race

---
**Flesh Races** is a term used for races that are built by accumulated masses of exposed flesh.
The exposed part of flesh hardens, but still reacts in similar manner as it would normally upon
damage. Flesh races however gained ability of the flesh to regrow, which explains the existence
of layered structure of their bodies.  
Entities that are less flesh-grown are an example of being fortunate to not get themselves hurt, 
but the hurt itself and regrowing of flesh is also a cultural part that is considered a passing 
ritual, establishing one's maturity, later becoming a tool or expression of one's experience.

### Flesh Cave
| ![](/Assets/Culture/Architecture/flesh_cave.png)           |
|------------------------------------------------------------|

Flesh caves are structures built by maita and tsisu that is meant to provide protection to them.
Approaching cave suitable for shelter, they start to scrap their flesh out of their bodies and
sculpt with it cave insides. The material adapts quickly, and with enough of it, it gains some
sort of consciousness. This makes flesh cave react to its environment, basically providing
maita and tsisu protection through its various traps. Flesh cave can claw, gnaw or even devour
anyone coming to the cave. The eaten flesh of the prey can also expand living cave parts 
slightly further.

The process of flesh cave expanding is slow, so most of them are only partially covered. Caves 
covered fully tend to be built by oldest groups or be ones that defended themselves from big
groups of enemies.  
Flesh cave can exist even without its original inhabitants - this however renders them hardest
to control, since they can only be guided by flesh races from which skin they were created.
