Ghlodds are one of the oldest civilisations in Baedoor universe, inhabiting enormous part of [Seiteh](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Seiteh.md)
continental island, including both surface and deep cave system below it.  

Ghloddish bodies are heavily deformed, from some unknown reason dating back to oldest times,
sometimes connected to them living under the surface for so long.  
While this could be true considering some ghlodds have indeed even deformed eyes, this doesn't stand
true for broader population where the only casualties are limbs - most frequently non-existent legs
and short, malformed arms.  
This phenomenon isn't making ghlodds any way less potent, though: their race developed some psychical
power to move their bodies through intense will. The same power allows them to more easily hold any
items in their arms, despite lesser energy being theoretically given due to shorter limbs.  
This goes quite far when we also see technological advances of ghlodds: a lot of technology and
advancements in metalurgy (including Seiteh being biggest deposit of cirtain metal) makes them quite
well with overcoming their limitations of body.

Ghloddish culture was evolving through all thousand years of universe's life, from simple clan-driven
civilisation, through aggressive Empire, to now place to-be-cosmopolitan-centre of this part of universe.  
Despite all those changes, ghloddish culture stayed surprisingly intact, still a bit hermetic,
traditionalist-yet-enlightened, faithful to their spiritual roots.  
They are known to follow their specific, quite complex societal system, which is guided both by
religious tales and value given to family and community. A lot of philosophic part of ghloddish
mindset is driven also by softly seen romanticism, embracing their primal hot-headedness and love for war.  
Despite this, peaceful values tend to be embraced just as heavily, and often those more chaotic powers
are said to be used in science and art, being gift from ghloddish gods to the mortals.
