**Zhi** (sometimes referred as _nightsky-kin_ or less correctly as _opposite elves_) are race
native to **West Moon** and **Central Moon**, though they are prevalent less commonly very
far from their origin.

Zhi are black-skinned, with glimmering translucent-whitish star-shaped spots dotting their
skin in myriad of ways, unique to each zhi.

### Mythological origins
According to the **Lai-Kine** narratives, zhi is a second creation of **Laitalu** after **(luna)tri**.
They were woven out of the night sky, hence the darkness of their skin and the star-shaped spots
(not reminiscent of any actual night sky system however, and it is unknown what meaning these
spots have, if any at all).

Zhi are one of the races most commonly seen outside their territories, not feeling the belongingness
to any place. They are notorious travellers, sailors, tramps, depending on individual situation.
This attitude make them usually skilled in variety of crafts, hence they tend to be hired for
various tasks requiring knowledge or flexibility regarding transport.

As eternal travellers, zhis rarely talk, focused on being the silent background, listening and
learning in more passive ways. That said, if approached in proper way, they will uncover mysteries
learned during their journeys and teached by the echos of the void, stars, and the occult
knowledge known only between Laitalu and them.

Despite that common appearance as always-moving, zhis are also equally settled. A lot of them
find sense and wisdom in sticking to the surroundings, either of their birth or ones they found
peace in. The sense of detachment from others is shared between settled and wandering ones, uniting
them despite distance. The characteristic trait of zhi as a whole is their extreme assimilation,
but one that is distant and can be disconnected at any time. They are rather invisible cogs of the
society rather than participants.

Zhi communicate with the same language as tri, being shared and unchanging tongue of all Laitalu
creations.
