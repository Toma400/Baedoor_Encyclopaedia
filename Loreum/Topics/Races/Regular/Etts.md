# 💮 Etts

---
**Etts** is a race of ancient builders, sharing **Rossevette** together with **latoka**. Heavily
invested in metallurgy, biology and technology, they built innovative society that thrived thanks
to automation and experiments on environment.

In old times, ettic society existed on two spheres - the common folks lived in villages, primarily
of agrarian character; the elites resided in giant strongholds where they reigned over villages
through their knowledge.  
Ettic elites however were built on merit, as they consisted primarily of scientists and technologists,
the ones who could offer actual services to villages they were lords of. Aside of being part of that
mutual dependency, elites had not much say on villages' life, as the rest of it was detached from
the lone lords, including the army that was built grassroots. Elites were basically a banished
ones, but ones that were able to buy into the favour of villages.
