Ormaths are race of short, gray-skinned beings, gathering together in clans spread around desert
lands of [Arennan](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Arennan.md). They live as nomads, 
rarely settling in one place for longer. They live in leather tents placed on circle plane, with 
holes at the roof center - used to let smoke flow without smoking out the room. The hole quite often 
uses leather web to cover empty space a bit.

Ormath clans are ruled by the elders, however on top of them there's always shaman who has definitive
word on everything. He is considered to be ruler, father and religious leader doing rituals.  
He also has exclusive right to enter bigger tent, being sanctuary for wind gods, following which is
main ormath's religious pillar.
