# 💮 Apta

**Apta** are race of semi-humanoidal creatures with their skin partially made out of lava-like
material. This makes their kind situated somewhere in between regular and elemental race. It's
uncertain how much they are connected to [flesh races](../Flesh_Race.md) inhabiting the same 
territories, but there's a link suggesting apta skin evolved from flesh bodies of that group.

Their organism provides them full resistance from heat, meaning that even while catching fire, the
burning doesn't hurt them internally, only consuming the skin. The skin however tends to regenerate
even from full erasure, so any damage to it is only temporary.

Apta are race of technologists who, thanks to their abilities, allowed them for particularly
specific experiments and research. Many aptas live deep underground or in spaces of excessive heat,
using materials available in this space.  
Aptas are the ones who, in the early times of their Empire, dominated the trade of lava-made
accessories, primarily weapons. Jitadian technologies relied heavily on lava work transited
through Mes Inpea.

### The Shining Empire
Apta in Pre Era occupied vast territories of Vershaton, at the time a place more lush and fertile.  
Its cities were big and monumental, dotting the area from southern coast to the northern one.  
With capital city of Mavvarad, located around the central vulcano, the power of Apta grew to the
point of becoming one of bigger singular powers of the area - creating what was called at the time
The Shining Empire.

### Red Garden
Apta, being heavily underground race and one that had a lot of their mythology revolving around
the Underground World, had shaped the concept of Red Garden.  
**Red Garden** is a name for idyllic world located deep underground, a gigantic fortress with
lush interior able to fit all the apta inside, keeping them in state of eternal fulfillment
of any needs. In most common understanding, Red Garden was meant to be built, not found.

The concept of Red Garden was approached many times by most skilled technologists and visionnaires.  
Its prototypes were built in the vulcano of Vershaton, funded by the emperor residing in Mavvarad.

### Trivia
Apta are repurposed race that was meant to live in Rossevette's mountain region under the name
of etts. After some brainstorming though it was decided that this region is not particularly
fitting and also saturates the area too much, while Vershaton in the other hand does lack some
depth and reasoning behind the wastes located there.

The name for Red Garden comes as a homage to MammaHyena's "Vvredgarden", taking also some
thematic inspirations of post-cataclysmic area.
