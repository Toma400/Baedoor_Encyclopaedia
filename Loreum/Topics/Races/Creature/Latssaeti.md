# 💮 Latssaeti

| Latssaeti in Baedoor Classic                          |

**Latssaeti** (pronounced *latseti*, from nomath. *latšsu*) are flesh golems, the biggest of [flesh races](../Flesh_Race.md).
Due to their size, they primarily use their strength and oversized tools to manipulate environment.  
Combination of Latssaeti's wild look and size makes them most feared creature, even more so than
goblins.

They live in mountains, often joining maita, tsisu or goblin communities, providing ourselves mutual
support. It's rather rare to found them in quantity bigger than one at a time.
