# 💮 Stheyr

**Stheyr** is creature race living in deserts of [Vaerdena](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon/Lands/Vaerdena.md).

Stheyrs are sentient creatures living in self-made sand caves buried under the surface, made by male
workers out of sand hardened with their mucus produced by their thorax.  
Stheyr caves are usually not particularly deep, containing up to few rooms and going horizontally rather
than vertically. Rooms are used primarily to sleep and breed, but some of them can have shelves carved
to store food or other items.

Stheyrs move on their four limbs, however they can differ between legs and arms and stand for specific
tasks - such as placing their resources on cave shelves or breeding.  
Stheyrs are very fast and dangerous fighters - they do move quickly and use both their claws and sharp
teeth to rip the enemy apart. They are also fairly intelligent when it comes to movement.

Stheyrs life cycle revolves around breeding and eating, to which their organisms evolved perfectly to.
They can eat anything, including carcasses which eventually end up being their main source of food on 
such wastelands. Their stomach digests food in very efficient way, meaning they can live without
eating for few days and keep most of their energy. While they usually eat other creatures, cannibalism
is a regular experience of theirs.  
Stheyrs do breed excessively, without any care about sex partner. Whenever stheyr feels lust, they
initiate sex with first stheyr they meet of opposite sex. It is not rare that stheyr even breed with
pregnant ones, which ironically enough only fastens the process, relaxing the organism.
The pregnancy of stheyrs take only few weeks, with female individuals laying egg at the end - if they
are bred during that time, it can happen even in half of that period.

Stheyrs hatch in few days after egg is laid, after which young ones are left somewhat alone.
They learn basics by themselves and looking at older creatures. That's also how [tertens](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/Tertens_Vaerdena/[Culture].md)
usually enslave stheyrs to use as a transport - by killing the group and bringing young stheyrs to be
trained to specific purpose.

Stheyrs do not live particularly long - their usual life lasts from four to six years, however usually
it ends much earlier due to danger or other groupmates.  
It is unclear if stheyrs have any form of religious beliefs, as there's no material culture that would
indicate so. That said, what could be perceived as ritual is stheyr burials - once one of stheyrs die,
other stheyrs call each other to feast on its body.
