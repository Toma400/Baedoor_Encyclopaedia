# 💮 Talaaxi

**Talaaxi** are creature race resembling apes, found on [Ixathei](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Ixathei.md).
They are heavily intelligent, combining their technological achievements with following ways of 
nature, building constructions and tools that let them rather integrate with environment than 
separate themselves from it.  
Talaaxi have established great connection with creatures and are skilled with taming them, using
for various purposes. The art of taming is very individual, as creatures are also seen by Talaaxi
as equal beings who are a link to gods, meaning the whole process holds personal significance.
The deities of Talaaxi have also a lot of features derived from local creatures, most notably birds.
The religion itself is somewhat formal, but also colourful, expressive and has ascensive manner.

They are rather proud, which can be seen through their monumental architecture. In their native
areas ancient dams, viaducts and gardens can be found, maintained for centuries since their
original builders.

### Kaer and Saphtri Invasion
As great technologists, Talaaxi were aware of far away lands such as Kaer or Arennan. While not
particularly expansivists, they researched surrounding areas and established multitude of sailing
connections, for trade or resource gathering.  
At one point in Pre Era, Talaaxi organised a grand colonisation campaign, meaning to settle on Kaer
and research its lands that they found similar, yet wildly different. The campaign succeeded and
let Talaaxi exist on Kaer for a good while - their attitude towards nature yielded them relative
favourness of local spirits, helping them to establish many settlements without pushback.  
Situation changed with goblin blood tribes earning favour of more powerful spirits that together
wiped out Talaaxi on the island, either by killing them or possessing them.

The second relationship Talaaxi had with Baedoor Continent's cultures is the saphtric campaign
on the west - which, however, yielded itself unsuccessful, concluding on primarily many naval
battles and few defences of Talaaxi coastal port cities.
