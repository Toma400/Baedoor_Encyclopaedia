Goblins are creature race inhabiting [Baedoor continent](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent.md),
specifically islands near Rossevette and Kaer. Their population spanned across Baedoor island and
Rossevette too, but it decimated heavily due to baedoorians' conquests.

---
♜: they were befriended by nomathians and used in their army, thus when fighting baedoorians, they
were considered equal enemy, but much more primitive and exterminated more mercilessly
