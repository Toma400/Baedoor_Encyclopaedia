# 🎇 Baedoor Railroad Enterprise

---
**Baedoor Railroad Enterprise** is company founded in 701 OE by [Anaurin Ettua](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Ettua_Anaurin.md)
and [Natem Calgori](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Calgori_Natem.md), with its residence found in
[Baedoor City](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Baedoor.md). Ever since its
founding, BRE became the major (and for long time, the only) company offering infrastructure
services for railroad systems, including logistics and production.
