# 🎇 Flamecoat Bank

---
Bank founded by [Flamecoat family](../../Characters/Families/Flamecoat.md) in 277 OE. Recognised
as the oldest still working bank of Baedoor state, with extensive branches spanning between [Rossevette](../../Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Rossevette_Islands.md), 
[Kaer](../../Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer.md), [Arennan](../../Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Arennan.md)
and **West Baedoor**.
