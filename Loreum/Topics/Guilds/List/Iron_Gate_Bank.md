# 🎇 Iron Gate Bank

---
Bank with the origins in [Skirran](../../Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Skirran.md),
operating on northern parts of East Baedoor state and neighbouring colonial states. Founded in
309 OE, it's considered one of the oldest still running banks.
