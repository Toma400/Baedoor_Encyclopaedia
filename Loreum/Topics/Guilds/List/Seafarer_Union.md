# 🎇 Seafarer Union

---
A union of **Kelvos** guilds, including ones of sailors, traders and the remainders of now-defunct
**Brotherhood of the Coast**. Founded in 115 NE, the Union was meant to be all-encompassing
institution that would formalise various actions and demands brought by guilds towards the
Kelvos city-state and neighbouring factions.

In 244 NE, the Union established [Seafarer Union Bank](Seafarer_Union_Bank.md), further expanding
its significance in the area.
