# 🎇 Ansur Thieves Guild

---
**Ansur Thieves Guild** is guild institution of pretty long history, forging [Evros](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon/Cities/Evros.md)
trade for hundreds of years.  
Unlike other Thieves Guilds, Ansur one isn't associated with any other one, and also does not 
follow traditional robbery-focused ways of this art. Just as all guilds on [Ansur](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon/Ansur.md),
it is much more politically bound, and as such, ATG controls trade with its mafia-like influences.
Guild's life is based upon smuggling forbidden goods in and out, but also on selective protection
and having actual ship fleet to press pirates to respect ATG's status.  
A lot of Ansur's Thieves Guild existence is intertwined with silent support given from officials and
Ansur Navy, as it allows the hand of law resolve more delicate situations without dirtying their hands.

Aside of Evros, ATG supports financially **The Blossoming Lavender** guild in **Waine**, however
their presence in the city is sporadic. It has small presence over [Nyme](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon/Cities/Nyme.md)
given its loose restrictions over the trade, however ATG does not use this potential to not step
too much on government's toes.

Current ATG's chef is [Ferves Navikon](../../Characters/List/Navikon_Ferves.md), old lady
known for her still remarkable shooting skills.

### History
ATG arose from group of lower class lawless citizens early in Ansur's colonisation, trying to help
each other to protect from the law and to establish a black market trade. Early ATG was therefore
primarily focused on finding places to safely do the deals between members. From this time 
arises the informal statute that hold loyalty high and enforced penalty on thief who would 
collaborate with the guards.

Over the next decades, ATG also built its small hierarchy system that ensured the statute is held
accountable and later, to have a web of agents informing about moves of government that could
interfere with ATG members' interests. This also eventually led to establishing the role of
ATG chef, who would navigate the organisational aspect of the guild. The chef would be similarly
accountable for working against guild's interest.  
At this point, Baedoor Empire also enforced much higher bounty on ATG, making the guild seen
as main threat of Ansur. The organisation thus had to not behave too boldly, to not bring too
much attention to itself - meaning the focus of the guild changed to more hidden actions and
rather interference over politics than direct lawbreaking.

Things changed however with **Norran Gorth** supporting Ansur's independency. ATG saw the 
opportunity in overturning falling Empire's law and so supported Norran candidature, introducing
what will later become a tight interrelationship between the guild and Ansur's government.
