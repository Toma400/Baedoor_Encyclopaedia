# 🎇 Order of the Crossroads

---
An art-political secret society, formed mostly of [Theatre of Grey Mask](../../Cultures/Baedoorians/List/Theatre_of_Grey_Mask.md)
actors. Its ideological goals is public access to culture and consciousness of the masses.

Members of the guild are involved in variety of propagandist actions, drawing attention to their
matters through public performances.  
Order has also its own printing press, semi-legally reprinting books that either went out of use, 
are niche, or serve political consciousness (either as a propagandist tool or exposure of opponents).
