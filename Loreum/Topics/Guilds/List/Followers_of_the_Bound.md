# 🎇 Followers of the Bound

---

<img alt="FotB emblem" src="/Assets/emblem_followers_of_the_bound.png" width="400px"/>

**Followers of the Bound** is guild of mages exclusive to [Ansur](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon/Ansur.md).    
It follows a distinct magic style compared to other guilds and organisations - focusing on casting
spells onto items.
This type of magic is usually considered difficult, unpredictable and rather unproductive
compared to other methods, but Followers find it as their small area of love they want
to practise and study.

The guild originates from few magicians who, living in Evros, found that they all tend to experiment
with their craft, and thus formed a friendship to support each other with research. The bond
formalised to a guild, as this helped to gather financial support from town hall for more expensive
equipment. This also brought more members and further set formal structures in.

On 426 NE, **Irlan Lavago** became a master of the guild. While being fairly young, he is 
accustomed to ways of FotB works and also is an experienced mage with fair amount of knowledge.
Irlan practices include experimenting with ghloddish sigils.

Their symbol is a staff with a purple veil.
