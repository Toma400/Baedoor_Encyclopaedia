# 🎇 Guild of Adventurers

---
Existing for two centuries small guild located in [Alkaesar](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Alkaesar.md)
and [Morpat](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Morpat.md), founded
by three immigrants from [Mes Inpea](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Old_Continent/Mes_Inpea.md).

### History
Guild's origin goes back to group of mesinpean immigrants travelling to [Skirran](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Skirran.md).
They headed south, seeing interest many had in Vershaton. Three friends stopped at Alkaesar, where 
they came up with the idea of a service providing goods for adventurers trying to go further into 
Vershaton and supply themselves with necessary tools, food and other measures.

This is when Guild started - while in reality nothing more than just a shop with snobbish title, 
the name worked and quickly the guild earned enough money for one of its founders suggesting expanding
its services to Morpat - so the whole passage to Vershaton is covered.  
The guild itself expanded its services to even host a small sleeping room and had relatively 
successful time throughout initial several years. As the interest in Vershaton declined, the guild's 
interests stopped being as profitable, yet still remaining relatively stable.

The guild achieved to live throughout four generations in Alkaesar, with final owner moving to Skirran.
Radpat's guild was bought much earlier after its owner's death.
