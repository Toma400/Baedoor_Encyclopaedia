# 🎇 Seafarer Union Bank

---
Bank located in Kelvos that was created upon initiative of [Seafarer Union](Seafarer_Union.md)
in 244 NE. It first handled matters of sailors of the area, but eventually expanded to **Agoi** as
part of diplomatic efforts.

### 🔻 BRANCHES
- Kelvos
