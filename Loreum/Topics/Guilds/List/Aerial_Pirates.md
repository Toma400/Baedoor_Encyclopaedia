# 🎇 Aerial Pirates

---
**Aerial Pirates** are name for various military groups in Arennan that existed there ever since
constructing knowledge of [saphtri flying ships](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/Tri_Saphtris/Technologies.md)
became more common among saphtris. Originating from a bit richer bandit groups roaming Arennan, pirates
decided to expand their practice to attack their targets from above.  
Aerial pirates were known for their skills to build ships and launchers incredibly fast, which makes
them much more portable fighters. While the ships they made were usually primitive and flawed, it 
risked great danger to any travellers.   
As a dangerous threat, they were targeted a lot by Arennan state, with varied efficiency. 

Their role changed with **Baedoor Empire** invading Arennan. Even as outcasts, pirates felt responsible
for their lands and hated occupants often no less than citizens.  
A lot of pirate groups fought together with other terrorist groups as sort of guerilla forces.

After peace of treaty in 1018 OE, the rulers of Arennan offered amnesty to plenty of pirates groups,
sometimes also including for their pilots to be hired among army forces. Fair amount of pirates used that
opportunity, with remaining groups still living from brigandage to this day.
