# 🎇 Desert Divisions

---
A military unit, made in 930 OE in an effort to keep conquered Arennan areas under Empire's control.
It was made out of **Third Grand Fleet** forces and had similar structure, although troops experienced
in desert and anti-guerilla fight were prioritised.

In 935 OE, general Norran Mevren decides with other generals working with him currently on slaughter 
of Circle of Blood Swords. Warthog grants them permission, and the massacre begins.  
This action brings extreme outcry among Arennan population, counting both leaders and commonfolks 
and radicalising all liberation groups. Few months after, Norran Mevren is assassinated, and saphtric 
patriots attacks become more frequent.

Desert Divisions are disbanded during Tom Varteford's reign in 1018 OE, as a part of peace treaty.
