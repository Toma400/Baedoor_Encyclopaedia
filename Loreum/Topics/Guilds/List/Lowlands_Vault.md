# 🎇 Lowlands Vault

---
Low-income commoner bank operating in the giant lowlands, heavily used by farmers, traders and
citizens of the area. While broadly available in the heart of the western half of the island, it
does not have strong significance for the wider state.

### 🔻 BRANCHES
- Quarius
- Sargin
- Tallamit
- Maranon
- Delleret
- Heraille
