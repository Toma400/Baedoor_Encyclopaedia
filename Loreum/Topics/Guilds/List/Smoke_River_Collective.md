# 🎇 Smoke River Collective

---
**Smoke River Collective** is an unofficial group of **Sargin University** students, primarily
from humanities. Appearing loosely around 420 NE, it first was a group of friends fascinated by
romantic ideas of [Souvie Darakon](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Darakon_Souvie.md), creating
poetry and art dedicated to concepts of transcending the self by aspiration and sacrifice
to ideas, with strong emphasis on love and community.  
SRC members at the time lived cosy, somewhat hedonistic life heavily surrounded by various small
rituals of the group, often meeting after lessons or in private.

At the end of group's university period, SRC grew ever so slightly with some students from earlier
years befriended and continuing the traditions. Founders themselves, albeit no longer connected
to the university, sometimes organise meetings in their homes - albeit these are mostly visited
by older SRC members, basically splitting original SRC and younger SRC into two groups that only
sometimes intertwine, yet are faithful to similar spirit.

### Regular days & rituals
SRC symbolism and rituals were various, playing loosely with their philosophies, hedonistic
approach to life and shared friendships.

Their most common place to meet was at the bank of the river crossing Sargin, as oftentimes
(especially during autumn and winter) evenings after lessons turned into nights quickly, bringing
atmosphere emphasizing the ambience.

One of the most known traditions was sharing **cigarettes** with a friend, which was considered
not as just a friendly gesture, but a kiss with the one who the cigarette was shared - be it a
sign of deeper connection or romantic attraction. This was not an uncommon way to show someone
initial sympathy, as few more shy couples revealed their feelings to each other that way.
