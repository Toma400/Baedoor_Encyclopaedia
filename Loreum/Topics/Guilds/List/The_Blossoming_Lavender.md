# 🎇 The Blossoming Lavender

---
**The Blossoming Lavender** is informal organisation established in **Waine** in 243 NE, by group
of citizens with **Sara Lensati** as an initiative founder. Its mission is to offer a shelter and
food for the homeless of the city, for which Lensati offered one of her tenements which was later
held by next organisations' stewards.

Due to little restrictions of Blossoming Lavender's ways of operating, the tenement is frequented
also by more shady individuals seeing opportunity to perform illegal trades in there using crowded
place as a cover. This results in not uncommon guard intrusions during investigations related
to drug smuggling or other similar crimes. Over the years however, guards try to perform more
secret operations in cooperation with Blossoming Lavender's management.

Organisation's iconography showcases lavender flower, as a symbol of individual among the masses.
Lensati had particular sweet spot to lavender fields that she was seeing during her youth on nearby
farm, and that she connected to beauty and growth. She suggested the symbol as a way to empower
the ones that had no support, drawing connection between each single person seeking help and the
unity that could be found between homeless groups seen within the city - and people who were willing
to take the initiative further.

Currently, Blossoming Lavender leadership counts three people: a young redhaired lady,
**Laika Narras**, an old philanthropist **Facon Tarlae** and **Minne de Harat**, an aristocrat
lady married to one of Waine's richest traders.
