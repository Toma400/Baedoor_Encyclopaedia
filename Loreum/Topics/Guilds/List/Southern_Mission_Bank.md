# 🎇 Southern Mission Bank

---
A jointed bank of three southern cities, founded in late OE as a result of conquests of the time.
The bank built its strength on loans for families trying to enrich themselves during that period;
in New Era it primarily serves as southern coastal bank of established position.

### 🔻 BRANCHES
- Ontero
- Fressi
- Pervesi
