# 🍁 Unteupta Assassination
Assassination done on 12th November 999 OE, on military airship [Unteupta](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/[Vehicles]/List/Unteupta.md).
In early morning hours, Unteupta started from Tharie port. Airship was transporting group of esteemed
generals, ending their year-end campaign on Arennan lands.

Four hours after airship flew off, saphtric [separatists](/Loreum/Topics/Factions/List/Separatists.md)
attacked the airship with their fast, small fighter planes. In the same time, onboard engineer
supporting liberation fight set up bombs inside the airship.  
Unteupta was torn apart with the explosion, killing almost entirety of transported people.
