# 🍁 Varteford's Peace Treaty
**Varteford's Peace Treaty** is a name for the treaty that established new administrative order
of **Baedoor Continent** for most of its states, as an outcome of long fights of **Separatists**
and a way to resolve crisis and unrests in the Empire's heartlands.  
Signed by [Tom Varteford](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Varteford_Tom.md) on 11th June 1018 OE...

### Outcomes for Rossevette
Baedoor Empire's good relationships with latoka helped to achieve fairly satisfying borders
for latoka state compared to what voitric state required. Although not made without haste and
skipping local concerns, drawn along the Sekhorot river, it helped to protect latoka through
environmental means. In the same time, latoka also reestablished the old realm system that
proved to be more effective in many fields.  
In the same time, voitri received fair amount of border lands of latoka, most notably huge chunk
of ettic realm (under the reasoning of protecting their land from ettic raids) and Nukrat-Liim
realm that voitri particularly absorbed during their previous occupation, getting rid of its
Joyous population.
