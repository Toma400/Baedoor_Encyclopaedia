**Ascaron** is one of latoka **realms**, encompassing primarily **Emerald Hills region** with
its [major settlement of the same name](../../Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Rossevette_Islands/Cities/Ascaron.md).

Ascaron is ruled through its long-established kingship and king-elected representatives, serving
as a proxy to maintain relationship between king, guilds, population, other factions and eventually
being king's voice in **councils**. That entangled internal structure web makes for efficient
power that however is quite resistant from changing course, being heavily reliant on the old
leader himself.

The king's relationship to **West Baedoor** is a known point of contention among lower upper class
(primarily guilds) that lean into more isolationist approach. King's court however for long time
emphasizes trade and technology exchanges with former Empire as a way to ensure realm's growth.  
This approach helped Ascaron to be received as a partner state during Empire's occupation rather
than entirely occupied territory. The continued policy after Treaty on the other hand ensured the
faithful support and connections between two cultures, benefiting broader latoka community.

Ascaron holds de facto sole voice in **Upper Ascaron** coalition, making its position fairly
uniform across United Council. Ascaron is known to be representative of **West Baedoor**, having
the closest ties to it and uses that often as a leverage between coalitions.  
Those close ties however also build distrust and an image of realm being foreign puppet, so 
representatives of Ascaron oftentimes dance between interests to dismiss such concerns.

### Realm spirit
The general idea of cosmopolitanism comes from two sources: first, the realm being relatively
younger from its neighbours, being built on top of older realm that ceased to exist. That brought
the state a sense of being a fringe realm, waking sense of rivalry and building its importance.
That emphasis on interstate relationships remained as more or less emphasized principle of the
ruling class throughout entire time of realm's existence.  
A second reason behind cosmopolitanism is also that the realm in itself couldn't build its
strength through local resources or internal politics alone, being relatively secluded between
dense forests, mountain chain and sea. Trade built huge part of Ascaron's economy and even
more importantly brought a lot of interest on services, expanding its offer and abilities with
technological prestige.
