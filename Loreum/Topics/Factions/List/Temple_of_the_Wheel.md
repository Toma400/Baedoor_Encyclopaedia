# ⚜️ Temple of the Wheel

**Temple of the Wheel** is name of old religious organisation that serves erdic society to
follow the proper rites of their "garden" faith-philosophy.  
Unlike regular religious organisations, Temple is aimed only on administrative tasks, such as
organising burials, providing extensive historical list of erds buried in the local garden
graveyard and taking care of both its temples' outside and inside area.  
Back in time, temples also had its own area dedicated to rewriting philosophical scriptures, but
with printing press being introduced during baedoorians' occupation, this work was entirely taken
by local printers - with temples either leaving those rooms empty or repurposed. The only temple
still maintaining old rewriting tradition is found in [Disanteldir](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer/Cities/Disanteldir.md)
due to lack of printing press in the town.

As service in Temple is considered voluntary and unpaid, its priests and guards are paid by the
governing body taking care of local area.
