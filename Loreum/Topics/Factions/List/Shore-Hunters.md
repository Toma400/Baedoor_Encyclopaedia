**Shore-Hunters** is an old, rural realm located in the valley between **Ilumat Highlands** and
**Dewehr Mountains**. It is a home to local latoka fishermen, farmers and miners spread around
few villages occupying the place.  
Despite being a poor state, the realm maintains somewhat fertile areas, being main food supporter
of neighbouring **Likkrot-Nanat**.

As Shore-Hunters community is built by workers, there is no inherent structured power dynamics 
characterising other latoka realms. Representatives chosen for councils are selected among each
village. That rotating system however usually results in low effectiveness on diplomatic field, as
it tends to select members without any political experience.
