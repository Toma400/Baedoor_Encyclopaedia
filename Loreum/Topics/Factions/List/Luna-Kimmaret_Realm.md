**Luna-Kimmaret** is small realm made of jointed latoka realm of **Kimmaret** and nearby pahtric
**Luna village**. The connection happened by the time of rise of **voitric faith**, when the
believers of different Lai-Kine order started to remove **The Joyous** pahtri, forcing them to
migrate or changing faith. Luna inhabitants then decided to join more open-minded latoka state to
gain protection. Since then, it is frequent destination to the leftovers of the faith in the
region.

Currently, Luna-Kimmaret is one of the frontier realms, but gained independency back as a result
of [Varteford's Peace Treaty](../../History/List/Varteford_Peace_Treaty.md) like most of
pre-occupation latoka realms.
