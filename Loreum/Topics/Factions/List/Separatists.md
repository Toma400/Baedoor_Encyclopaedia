# ⚜️ Separatists

**Separatists** is a consolidated name for various groups fighting with Baedoor Empire's occupation
of Rossevette, Kaer and Arennan during Old Era.  
While the name itself is pejorative, marking baedoorian claim of ownership as a desired default state, 
it became widely used due to range of Empire's influences. Natives of occupied states prefer to 
distance themselves from this word.

While this name for rebels is used more often than not and makes it look as if the liberating
efforts were a monolith, in reality there were countless groups of bigger and smaller importance 
that were part of Separatists, with also very different approach to their efforts.

### Arennan
Arennan was the biggest opposition of Empire's rule and also had the main role in successful pushback
during the Great Rebellion. Saphtric forces were fast and brutal, rather preferring well planned
military operation over diplomacy.

#### Circle of Blood Swords
Nationalistic saphtri group, existing as a political party in Arennan since late Pre Era.
Very vocal against Empire oppressors and radical in their moves.  
Due to their popularity, and military forces, Empire direct a slaughter of them under Warthog's rule,
leading to massive rise in power of other separatists' groups. Circle ceased to exist at that time, though.

#### Innis Amasi
Old patriotic family of great importance to saphtric culture, known for their care to preserve national 
culture and salvage their kind.
While not particularly inclined to war, they joined The Great Rebellion forces and put great stake 
investing heavily in an army back then.

#### Vas Sarai
Terrorist liberation group of saphtri, appearing after Empire conquered Arennan.  
It had minor support until the Circle of Blood Swords' slaughter, after which Vas Sarai became
the leading force against the Emperor. Their actions were done with extraordinary violence which
was contested even within other saphtric liberation groups.

### Kaer
Kaer resilience was for most its time prevailing as underground military and cultural actions
performed with mixed results. That was mostly a result of not enough proper organisation between
various groups and their sometimes conflicting interests.

#### Sain
The ancient militaristic erdic family, known for its relentless defence specifically
on southern front. Empire's occupation took its toll on their reputation, as their strength was
heavily challenged by lack of action.

#### Kehtic Guerilla
Several groups of mercenaries and specialist troops. Originally part of army (fighting erds before
Empire invasion, then later together against Empire), they went underground as soon as Empire 
took Kaer entirely and forced kehts to yield.

#### Tae Ravo Naehhag
Kehtic-serj organisation, built upon kehtic Tae Ravo and serj Naehhag groups.  
Their primary role was to exchange information and connecting various liberation groups via 
effective communication. They also organised infrequent sabotages. After Kaer restoring independence, 
it split up again into Tae Ravo and Naehhag.

### Rossevette
Rossevette during occupation was the most peaceful force of resistance, primarily being involved
in subverting the power from the inside.

#### Voi Netro (vtr. lit. "Black Path", symb. "Faithful Path")
Secret organisation built on aristocracy's concern of continued increased Empire's influence on 
Rossevette's society. Their way of fight was majorly gathering political power, usually in less 
noticeable ways such as mutual support and investing into particular social fields that were 
overfunded by Xavier or Calgori families from Baedoor.

#### Three Monocles
Baedoorian research group founded by three brothers, publicly known for their scientific work for 
Calgori family. In reality, the group was also supportive of liberating actions and helped those 
efforts via their connections and services, using fairly peaceful time during Kaer invasion.  
During interregnum and Separatists taking advantage of Empire collapsing, children of founders 
fled further inland, afraid of possible pogroms on baedoorians.
