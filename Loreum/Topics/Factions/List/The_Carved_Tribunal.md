**The Carved Tribunal** is historical faction constituting of three ettic kingdoms: **Leptu**, 
**Hinnut** and **Kahat**, established in the conception of ettic cultural consciousness.
Being heavily traditionalist theocracy, The Carved Tribunal is credited as major force
forging current ettic spirit - as well as creating an array of laws and rites used in there
still today.

The union appeared as a result of strengthening of voitric forces on the lowlands, rendering
ettic kingdoms more prone to unsuccessful raids and hence, starvation. The faction appeared
after diplomatic meetings, building tighter connection between kingdoms and its rulers.  
The existence of union helped with the regaining of communal solidarity of the ettic tribes,
bringing them together to restore the prior hereditary nature of their state. The theocratic
basis it was built upon will later restore the title of Ettic king after Tribunal's downfall.

With time and shaping up of **United Realms**, The Carved Tribunal became also a tool of
communication for ettic kingdoms with the broader latoka. That move let latoka realms ensure
safety from ettic raids in exchange for trade routes going through **Dewehr mountains**, tightly
protected by ettic forces.
