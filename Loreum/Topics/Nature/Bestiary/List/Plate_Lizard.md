# 🐊 Plate Lizard

---
**Plate Lizard** is a lizard type common to [Kaer](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer.md)
and islands close it.  
It is bigger from other lizard kinds and shielded with plates on its back. It's not aggressive towards
bigger creatures than itself, it eats mostly small creatures and various plants.  
It treats [Lhue](/Loreum/Topics/Nature/Herbarium/List/Lhue.md) as one of delicacies.
