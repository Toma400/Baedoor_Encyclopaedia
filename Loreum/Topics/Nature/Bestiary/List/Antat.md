# 🐊 Antat

---
**Antat** (ghl. *anta'th*) is a shellfish living in a shallow waters of [Seiteh](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Seiteh.md)'s
caves. It yields purple liquid able to be turned into dye, making its breeding a vital part for
colouring textiles and producing paint.
