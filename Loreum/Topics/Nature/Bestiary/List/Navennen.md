# 🐊 Navennen

---
**Navennen** is native flying creature to Baedoor island, a white, crow-like predator.  
Rarely seen, spends most of its life alone in the forests. Navennens can hunt far from their home, 
thus can be seen on fields searching for small creatures (such as rabbits, mouses, rats and others).

Navennens can be tamed only to the extent, as the possibility of them escaping is not unlikely and 
depends on each case.

The creature have small line shape around its eyes, usually being strong black colour with red 
or blue peeking through subtly.

For baedoorians, it is a symbol of determinance and expansion, which was also the reason behind 
Xavier family using it as their symbol.
