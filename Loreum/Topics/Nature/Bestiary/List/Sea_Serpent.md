# 🐊 Sea Serpent

---
**Sea Serpents** are fairly big sea creatures living on [Ennaeric Ocean](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/[Oceans]/Ennaeric_Ocean.md).
They range from length equivalent of two people, to one similar to small ship. Their body is reminiscing
of snake and covered with blue scales. They usually live for over hundred years.  
While they do breed once a year, their fertility is extremely small, meaning sea serpent can succeed 
at that statistically once in two decades, yielding up to two new serpents.

Sea serpents usually hunt and travel together in packs of four or five creatures. There's not really 
a possibility to see lone serpent, unless they get rest of their pack slaughtered or becomes haunted 
by terten spirit. Even sick serpents are not abandoned, and lone ones will try to find a group they 
can join into.

Serpents are intelligent, magical creatures, which means they are prone to be possessed by terten 
spirits wandering above waters.

### Tertenian Serpent
If [terten spirit](/Loreum/Topics/Races/Spirit/Terten.md) possess the serpent, it will naturally split
off its pack to serve the spirit residing in it. Over time, such serpents can transform their regular
scales into colourful ones, making them more recognisable to others (alongside the fact of wandering
alone).  
Tertenian serpents can be seen unfrequently near Arennan and Ixathei, where they are worshipped - by 
saphtris as spirit-guides, by ixatheians as water gods.
