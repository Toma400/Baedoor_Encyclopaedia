**Spectre Nosfer** is rare vampiric creature found in [Baedoor Island](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor.md)
forests. Sometimes classified as form of [terten](/Loreum/Topics/Races/Spirit/Terten.md), albeit
it is unclear how much its origin is taken from undead spirits.

Nosfers feast on bodies, dead or alive, drinking their blood. They aren't particularly strong nor
aggressive, but can hunt whenever they are hungry. Their primary attack involves paralysis, after
which nosfer can more easily chew on the body, letting blood flowing out weaken the prey even
further.
