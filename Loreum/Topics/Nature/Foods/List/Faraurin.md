# 🥮 Faraurin

![](/Assets/Nature/Foods/faraurin.jpg)
---
Faraurin is popular jitadian seasoning made out of crushed [???](). Its taste is unique combination
of salty and bitter taste, with moderate spice felt after a while.  
It is commonly used for fish, but jitadian citizen tend to experiment with it, so there are many recipes
using faraurin taste with even most strange combinations, including honey.
