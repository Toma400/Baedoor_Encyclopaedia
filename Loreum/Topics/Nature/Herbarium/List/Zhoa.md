# 🌿 Zhoa

---
| ![](/Assets/Sources/L_010_Egypt_Colony/arv_rossevette_10.jpg) |
|:-------------------------------------------------------------:|
|               Zhoa depicted in Baedoor Classic                |

**Zhoa** are native trees found in inner [Rossevette](../../../Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Rossevette_Islands.md),
primarily on western and northwestern coasts, less often deeper on island's interior. Zhoas don't
appear as often as other trees.

It is characteristic for being fairly tall and by their teal-coloured leaves.
