<code>Sketch of Lyrensi Purpui</code><br>

**Lyrensi Purpui** (sometimes referred as *Lyrensis Purpuris*) is flower found on Kaer island and south
of East Moon. It is liked by fireflies, thus making flower seem glowing in the night. It has very
delicate petals, reminding subtlety of the silk, which is unique property of Lyrensi.   
It is rather rare kind of flower.

Poisonous if eaten raw, however if prepared with enough care, it paradoxically can cure toxins. Similarly
to its petals though, preparation of Lyrensi Purpui is very difficult.
