# 🌿 Lyrensi Purpui

---

<img alt="Lyrensi Purpui" height="283" src="/Assets/Nature/Plants/lyrensi_purpui.png" width="153"/><br>
<code>Sketch of Lyrensi Purpui</code><br>

**Lyrensi Purpui** (sometimes referred as *Lyrensis Purpuris*) is flower found on [Kaer](../../../Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer.md).
It is liked by fireflies, thus making flower seem glowing in the night. It has very delicate petals, 
reminding subtlety of the silk, which is unique property of Lyrensi.   
It is rather rare kind of flower.

Poisonous if eaten raw, however if prepared with enough care, it paradoxically can cure toxins. Similarly
to its petals though, preparation of Lyrensi Purpui is very difficult.
