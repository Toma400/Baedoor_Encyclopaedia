**Tsua** (also called *tsue* by kehts) is plant native to Kaer. It has long, thin purple stem and
wide, purple-black petals, making "waves" around blue flower. Tsua emits weird smell, considered by
most as unpleasant.

It is wild plant, but is also sometimes cultivated, as its sap can be used as ink equivalent.

Tsuas are sometimes taken and dried up by young keht girls who use them as a crown. Dried tsuas lose
their awful scent, yet their colours remain almost as intense.  
Some kehts call tsuas "Star of Rising Day", as its blue colour reminds of sky right before sunrise.
