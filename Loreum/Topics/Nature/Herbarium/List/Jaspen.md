# 🌿 Jaspen

<code>Sketch of Jaspen</code><br>

**Jaspen** is quite rare flower living wildly on mountain ledges, quite often before lichen. It is
considered as pioneer plant and prefer heights. It blooms on February and lives to the end of the summer.
Can achieve height of maximum 10 centimeters.

It is highly poisonous for living beings. It has two variants, green one and even rarer red one - they
differ mostly by their side effects. Green one is hallucinogen, while red one causes paralysis.
