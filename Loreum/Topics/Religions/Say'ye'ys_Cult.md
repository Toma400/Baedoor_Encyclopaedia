# 🈳 Say'ye'ys Cult
**Say'ye'ys Cult** is offshoot of [Isheism](Tarrashe'_Ishe'.md), proclaiming [Say'ye'ys dragon](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Say'ye'ys.md)
as **Y'she'aur** messenger who was turned into dragon by **The're'sur** to withhold wisdom given
by the goddess.  
The cult worships the dragon as patron of art and the hidden truths, and is overall more invested
in magic and esoteric side of the faith from the regular religion.

Currently, the cult has minor representation in most of bigger cities on [Seiteh](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Seiteh.md),
particularly ones found underground. Say'ye'ys statues can be found in several cities, for example
[Sur Ta'hr](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Seiteh/Cities/Sur_Ta'hr.md).

### 🪔 HISTORY
Origins of the faith come from [Ghenash'r](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Seiteh/Cities/Ghenash'r.md),
where the Say'ye'ys is located. The dragon was seen by its citizens as mystical symbol even 
before forming of Isheism, albeit always as positive omen.
