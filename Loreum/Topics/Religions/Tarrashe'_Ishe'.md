# 🈳 Tarrashe' Ishe'
**Tarrashe' Ishe'** (ghl. *Tar'ashe'œ'Y'sheœ*, "travellers of the gods"), sometimes also referred
to as **Isheism**, is major [ghloddish](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/Ghlodds/[Culture].md) religion, 
being also one of the oldest known faiths in the universe still active to this day.

The idea of travel is crucial for ghloddish philosophy, as Isheism portrays gods as guides
(a term for god, `Y'she`, is translated literally to `the guiding being`). The main two myths
enclose the ghloddish journey, opening it with being guided by Y'she'sur, and closing with
guiding light of Y'she'aur.

### Deities
Isheism worships two gods, encompassing a binary constellation of a whole, which is seen through
the idea of their marriage.

**Y'she'sur** (guide-to-the-sun), a god of sun and day. A patron of work, journeys, exploration, 
research, change,
and dynamic elements of the world. Married to Y'she'aur, he is the male side of isheistic faith.
He is said to be wounded in the final battle according to the prophetic vision.  
His symbolic attribute is fire.

**Y'she'aur** (guide-to-the-moon), a god of moon and night, is a feminine side of Isheism. She is a patron of the
occult, maintenance, reflection, tradition, and things static. Married to Y'she'sur. She is
said to guide ghlodds home.  
Her symbolic attribute is water.

The theoretical opposites are shown here as a source of harmony and completeness, from which
comes the idea of both being necessary for healthy, fulfilling spirit.

### Origin Myth
At their inception, ghlodds lived under the caring arms of Y'she'aur (being now a reinterpretation
of Great Mother).
The time has come however where the Y'she'sur, living among ghlodd society in their form,
revealed himself and led ghlodds outside caves, onto the surface. Ghlodds went after the god-guide
without hesitation, and being blinded by the light for the first time, they began their journey
of self-discovery in this new world.

The event is sometimes also referred to as **The First Pilgrimage**.
