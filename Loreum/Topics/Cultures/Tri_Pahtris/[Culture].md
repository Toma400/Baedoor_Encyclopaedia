### Shadow Painters
Shadow painting is a name of a widely spread way of entertainment among pahtris and rarely
goblins, having its origins in goblins' shadow theatre.  
Pahtris have adopted shadow theatre during common encounters with goblin culture, and it became
a common practice among many, influencing pahtric culture from lowest to highest castes of society.
It also became popular way to indicate spiritual senses of The Joyous, as it helped shaping
this philosophy through simple, visual means. Skilled shadow actors were able to bring entire
scenes to the audience, giving such ephemeral stories a significant impact.  
**Shadow painting** became popular later on, as a way of magicians to collaborate with shadow 
actors. Mages let their magic colour what was previously just a shadow images, creating way more
complex and vibrant way to bring images to life. Mages who performed such illusionary colour 
follow-up to shadow actors were called shadow painters, and are popular to this day as 
either being hired by aristocrats, wandering from town to town, or as supporters of 
local religious entity. Some of them also visit goblin settlements, as goblins themselves find
this process as highly entertaining and are very welcoming to any shadow painter coming in.

Shadow painting became also an inspiration for baedoorians which gave life to first experiments
with photography in West Baedoor and attempts to create movies.
