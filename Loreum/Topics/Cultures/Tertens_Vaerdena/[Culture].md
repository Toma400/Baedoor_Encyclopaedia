# 🏺 Vaerdena Tertens Culture
Vaerdena tertens is civilisation existing for centuries on [Vaerdena Desert](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon/Lands/Vaerdena.md)
where they reign in form of city-states built on massive fortified mastabas of hardened sandstone.  
They do not maintain a state, but are rather a group of cities that are all independent of each other.

Vaerdena's tertens life revolves around spirituality and magical technology, making them very skilled
across multiple fields of research. 

### Form
Being [tertens](/Loreum/Topics/Races/Spirit/Terten.md) mean that their population does not grow naturally.
Vaerdena tertens come in variety of shapes and forms, but their main appearance is skeletons, zombies
and everything in between with bits of flesh staying in body.  
Tertens being undead mean they don't need to create food, making their cities self-sustainable and
being able to defend themselves without worry of starving population.

### Military
Tertens multidisciplinary skills can be seen in their army maintaining both magic and technology
to win over enemies.  
And tertens fighting is truly a sight to behold - aside of big army, they tend to support themselves 
with mages enchanting their projectiles, and huge walking undead turtles with harpoon turrets.  
That's why baedoorians entering the desert were terrified by sole look at their civilisation and
decided to not progress further south.

That being said, tertens do not try to expand and are rather defensive. The only situation they can
actually push for the attack is if they acknowledge already planned offensive and want to counter it
before it happens.  
While their approach is rather defensive, tertens do not like diplomacy - so all such efforts must be
done fast and effectively for diplomatic action to make sense.

### Architecture
Vaerdena tertens cities are build on massive sandstone mastabas, which allows them to have a lot of
tunnels and buildings inside of them, with various storages or even chapels found there.  
The city is split into districts, with quite rich variety of society's castes that have different
privileges in each, with priests and war commanders being able to access places not available to others.
Districts are usually separated by additional slabs put on top of the main city mastaba, with paths
forming naturally in between and stairs letting you get to the district.

### Religion
Tertens worship Gods of Destruction alongside their own saints, as their look at divine is not divided
into good and evil - they rather perceive things are intertwined unity.
