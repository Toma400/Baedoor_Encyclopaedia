# 🏺 Erdic Culture
Erdic culture has been built throughout millennia, as it is considered as oldest one on [Baedoor Continent](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent.md).  
It follows old philosophical idea of "the garden" that influences a lot of its aspects.  

Erds perfected their balance between beautiful ornamentation and simplicity, bringing artistry into
many fields of life in a way that is not overly extravagant.

### History
#### Beginnings
Beginning of erds as a nation and a community should start from the proto-erdic culture that
lived in **Kalas archipelago**, further east from Kaer. While having plain resemblances to current
erds, their culture differed and is now heavily lost in time. They were heavily stationary culture
however, not sailing far from their territories and doing so mostly for fishing.

Somewhen in Pre Era, a massive eruption of vulcano happened, drowning entire archipelago and
destroying the long-lived settlements built by proto-erdic community. A small group survived,
desperately sailing west, hoping their boats can survive despite not being accommodated for
deeper seas. Some of them got lost during that escape, but some stayed afloat long enough to
reach the beaches of eastern Kaer.

In that inhospitable place, proto-erds built first houses from anything they had. As their landing
spot have been almost devoid of any resources known to them, notably wood, the necessities of
survival shaped their initial experience that will inevitably remain to this day and build
foundation of the erdic culture and philosophy. The stone will become, aside of defensive material,
a one that shaped the spirit of resistance, and the first settlement built, **Sanaran**, will
eventually become a sacred place and a tragic scream to the lost past.

Eventually, in search of food, proto-erds created expeditions that went more inland - some going
south and north, and some trying to pass the big **Nemori range**. Contrary to expectations, the
one that survived was the one crossing the mountains - finding themselves in the lush forests of
**Eckhag** and **Marane**. Over next decades, erds started settling themselves along the Nemori
mountains, eventually reaching coasts - allowing Sanaran to connect by water. In that time also
**Detemardir** has been built, becoming first erdic capital, and the pass between it and Sanaran
became first monumental project tackled by the slowly emerging erdic state.

### Institutional Houses
Erdic culture holds the specific institution role, called *Houses*. Unlike actual institutions
and guilds, House is term used for structure built from incentive of individuals or local
society and has informal structure and laws, thus not fall within law sanctions beyond what
is permissible for everyone.  
That said, Houses can have charter, specific mission and be considered a legitimate 
partner, or even be funded by state or other donors.  
One of examples of such high legitimate House structure is **House of Restoration** found 
on [Kacari](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer/Kacari.md) which is House founded
upon the idea of researching history of the island and erdic culture. Being funded by both
[University of Illusion](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer/Places/University_of_Illusion.md)
and erdic state, it provides annual reports and has regular contact with donors, proving its
high trust status for its entire existence.  
Despite their general idea, Houses are usually seen as effective and trustworthy, as they
operate heavily on initiative of its members.

List of Houses:
- **House of Restoration** - organisation focused on exploring past of the Kacari island, 
                             independent, yet connected and funded by government and University of Illusion

- **House of Blue Bird** - an art organisation found in Roda's western district, funding independent 
                           artists and organising events of less known performers and musicians; 
                           their house is also a place of meetings for many lower-to-middle class
                           artists

- **House of Malachite Lantern** - organisation of magical research that does it on relatively
                                   small scale, but offers apprenticeship with much lower costs
                                   that University of Illusion; therefore is common destination
                                   of middle-class citizens
