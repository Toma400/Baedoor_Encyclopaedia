# 🎭 Erdic Art

### Music
The dominant instruments used by erds are rather soft and playful - they range from lyres, through
guitars, to bells (bigger and smaller) and triangles. They bring mesmerising, vibrative character
to places where they are played.  
Some modern instrumentarium that got imported during Empire occupation also found its place in 
erdic homes and inns, albeit is still seen as a curiosity.

Wind instruments (wind chimes, flutes) are considered philosophically and featured heavily in 
such contexts. They signify "breath" and so passing of future towards the past.  
The funeral ceremony is characterised by the procession that is guided by flutist - guiding the 
dead towards the temple garden. The ethereal sound dances on the streets, piercing the loudness 
of everyday life, clearing the way before the funeral crowd.
