# 🏯 Erdic Architecture
Erdic architecture is built upon the idea that home should be the garden in itself, and so they usually
offer separated semi-rooms and balconies outside, letting you rest outside, yet still within house's
area.

Typical erdic building follows fairly straight shape, with sloped roof covering it from rain and in
rare situations, snow.  
Houses are made primarily out of reddish-tinted wood common on erdic lands, but is oftentimes painted
with various colours, ranging from cream ones, to semi-dark red. Sometimes walls are painted with
some depictions of nature or have carved shapes - general aesthetics of erdic architecture come from
small details that are primarily seen by those inside "the garden".

One of characteristic features of Roda (but found also in other erdic cities) is pavement built from
colourful rocks. They are made in a way that embraces abstract shapes, but is not distracting.  
Sometimes this kind of path artistry can be seen also around old monasteries or shrines, deep in the
woods.
