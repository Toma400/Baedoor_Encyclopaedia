# 🏺 Ghloddish Culture
Ghloddish culture is surprisingly universal for race spread across island of continent size.
The biggest prevailing aspect of ghlodds existing in almost all areas is their attachment to tradition,
specifically such as local community, family and religious' tales and values.

In the most ancient time, ghlodds lived in the underground massive caves of [Seiteh](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Seiteh.md).
Their migration onto the surface was followed by the creation of [origin myth](/Loreum/Topics/Religions/Tarrashe'_Ishe'.md#origin-myth)
that brought them spiritual explanation of their past and present. Currently, ghlodds reside on both
planes, with their cities spanning across entire continent.

### Underground Ghlodds and Surface Ghlodds
As ghlodds split into ones that remained underground and those that started living on the
surface, they built an understanding of these societies as ones performing different
necessary work.  
Surface ghlodds are the enlightened by sun of Y'she'sur, and so are the workers of god, building
the understanding of the world and enhancing ghloddish kind. Their work builds their sense of
identity and destination - they will be taken back to the caves only once they are taken
prematurely (die) or when the prophecy is fulfilled.  
Underground ghlodds on the other hand are the maintainers. Their work is also seen as important,
as they lay ground for surface ghlodds when the prophecy happens. Underground societies are also
known to be more accustomed to esoteric knowledge, particularly one found in tradition or
ancient rites. The knowledge surface ghlodds bring will be the one of the world, while the
ones below keep the spiritual roots alive, ensuring they remain when time comes.

The split of roles is somewhat solidified for one's life, however there are cases of ghlodds
who migrate up or down - be it for entirely spiritual reasons or them finding different environment
more beneficial. Reasons are usually kept private, so there's no particular stigma towards
individuals - bigger migrations however can be seen as negative, especially if from already
emptying area.

### Religion
In the oldest times, before the origin myth with all its implications emerged, ghlodds lived in
underground mega-caves. Depending on tribe, they held belief in various spirits (usually locally
related to the mega-cave environment) and the idea of Great Spirit or Great Mother - an entity
that is superior over the small spirits and takes care of living beings.

As ghlodds migrated to the surface, their origin myth has been built, giving birth to the
[Isheism](/Loreum/Topics/Religions/Tarrashe'_Ishe'.md), being to this day the major ghloddish
religion. From this belief comes a lot of current ghloddish culture philosophy and thinking.

With time, various sects and religious offshoots emerged. That includes small subgroups like
[Say'ye'ys Cult](/Loreum/Topics/Religions/Say'ye'ys_Cult.md), but also heretical organisations 
like [Sect of Destroyer of the Sun](/Loreum/Topics/Religions/Sect_of_Destroyer_of_the_Sun.md).

Ghloddish burial practices remained the same throughout this entire time, being the one real
connection between old beliefs, Isheism and all other sectarian thoughts.  
Once dead, ghlodd must be returned to the cave, as they will not be able to return on their 
own during the promised prophecy. The ghloddish cemeteries are carved in cave's walls, consisting
of small, rounded rooms where the dead and few their belongings are put. They can reach ceiling
and span kilometers.
