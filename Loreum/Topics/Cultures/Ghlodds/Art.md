# 🎭 Ghloddish Art

### Music
Ghloddish music ever since its beginnings emphasized rhythm and was heavily built upon drum-like
instruments. This character however didn't make the ghloddish music heavy, as the supporting
techniques brought a balancing, ethereal ambient that made the rhythm feel somewhat faded in
instead of a central focus.

While ghloddish music feel directed towards ritualistic tone to foreigners, it does rather bring
a point of the strength that is distorted, a suppression of the difficult aspects of life - in a
way, it feels homely and communal.

Modern ghloddish music, in addition, experiment with technology and magic, producing unique
electronic-like sound. Some new genres emerged in ghloddish lands, being also a reason for new
cultural groups to gather around artists creating them.  
In some bigger cities, there are underground clubs fully dedicated to this kind of subcultures,
offering regular music nights with light shows. Such events are quite popular among young ghlodds,
making these genres grow in popularity.

### Illusion poems
Illusion poems, also spirit poems or poem of the spirit (_Earee_, from ghl. _ea'are'_) are a 
characteristic ghloddish poems written ever since early Pre Era.  
Written on the principle that "spirit does not need an excess", they are formed in a way 
to convey as much meaning in the shortest way. Hence, most of them are built on single words, and 
do not exceed beyond five, up to seven words.

There is no concrete criteria delineating whether the poem is illusion poem or not, it is generally
used term for compact poetry written in [ghloddish language](/Langue/Families/Ghloddish.md).
Main themes of the genre are feelings, existence, spirit and gods.

One of the most valued illusion poets is [Agha O'at'sa'](../../Characters/List/Agha_O'at'sa'.md).
