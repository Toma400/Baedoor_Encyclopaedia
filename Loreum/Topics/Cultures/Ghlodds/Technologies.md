# ⚗️ Ghloddish Technologies
Ghlodds are known to be marvellous technologists and their advancements are seen as one of the most
remarkable, setting them as masters of many crafts related to technology.

<table>
    <tr>
        <th colspan="2" style="font-size:1.5em">
            Flying Ships
        </th>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td width="50%">
            One of the most remarkable elements of ghloddish technology are their ships invented 
            as early as when they lived yet in underground caves of Seiteh.<br>
            Those flying ships use levitational engines that allow them to freely roam on low/middle 
            height through short distances.
            <br><br>
            Due to high flexibility, their flying ships became common way of transporting goods, army
            and regular citizens. They were equipped with various equipment that allowed them to 
            become one of the most powerful technological advantages over other nations.
