# ⚗️ Ghloddish Technologies
Ghlodds are known to be marvellous technologists and their advancements are seen as one of the most
remarkable, setting them as masters of many crafts related to technology.

### Flying ships

<div>
    <div align="left" style="margin-right:420px">
        One of the most remarkable elements of ghloddish technology are their ships that were invented
        during their oldest period when they lived yet in underground of Seiteh.<br>
        Their flying ships use levitational engines that allow them to freely roam on low/middle 
        height through short distances.
        <br><br>
        Due to high flexibility, their flying ships became common way of transporting goods, army and
        regular citizens. They were equipped with various equipment that allowed them to become one of
        the most powerful technological advantages over other nations.
