# 🏺 Strobe Music

---
**Strobe music** is a rhythmical, electrified music, created through magical operations on
metallic surfaces of [air whisperers](../../[Shared]/List/Air_Whisperer.md). The sound tends to be
draggy, reminding sometimes of whistle, but with ambience that creates heavier contextual
atmosphere. Not uncommonly, the rhythm becomes a major tool, setting the song to be more catchy
and danceable, inviting also more ritualistic character of traditional ghloddish music.

Strobe music originates from [Seiteh](../../../Geography/Seiteh.md) and is still exclusively
limited to [ghloddish](../[Culture].md) areas. Artists of this genre play in underground clubs 
across entire continent, but the genre hasn't become popular enough to leave its birthplace.
That might however be an outcome of it being still relatively new, as only rare mentions appeared
in new art magazines across oceans.

### Air gazers
While the majority of strobe music is played underground and confined to the interiors of
bigger cities, the cultural impact of strobe music became powerful to the point of some most
dedicated members of subculture finding relatively empty spaces - be it within the cities, or
in the deserts surrounding it - where they organise meetings and music raves, further building
community.

For young ghlodds, the genre became an escape and a way to find people of their kind. The idea
of "air gazer" was coined, signifying youth participating in such events.
