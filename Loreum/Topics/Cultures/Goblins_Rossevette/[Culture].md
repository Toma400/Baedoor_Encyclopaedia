# 🏺 Rossevette Goblins Culture
Rossevette goblins are culture of goblins that inhabits [Rossevette islands](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Rossevette_Islands.md).
They are known for their most static way of living out of all goblin cultures, as well as religious
beliefs heavily emphasizing natural phenomenons. A lot of cultural influences emerged from
goblins' common interactions with pahtris and latoka.

### Settlements
| ![](/Assets/Culture/Architecture/goblin_rosv_settlement.png) |
|--------------------------------------------------------------|

Rossevette goblins are the only ones that settle themselves in the wild, building their own
houses out of various materials (with heavy preference of wood, leather, thatch and alikes),
sometimes painted in colourful patterns.  
While their style is fairly simple, it has its practical edge.

The communal character of tribes allows houses to be fairly open, so the typical goblin 
settlement has walls primarily on the outer parts of the village. The closer to the center,
the more often visitor can see houses build entirely out of supports and minimal walling, 
allowing goblins to pass through.  
The open character helps with easy access to services and items at any time of day. There are
however personal alcoves that are walled, which shelter goblins from rain and allow them to
enjoy their private time with themselves.

The housing is focal part of the tribe's care of each its member, so whenever the child gets
mature enough to settle themselves, the whole tribe puts effort to provide them a home.  
Once built or found, the house belongs now to the goblin until their death or (incredibly rare)
events that would deprive them of them.
