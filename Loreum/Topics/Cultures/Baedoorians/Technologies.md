# ⚗️ Baedoorian Technologies

### Trains
| ![](/Assets/Culture/Vehicles/baedoorian_armoured_train.png)  |
|:------------------------------------------------------------:|
|                  Baedoorian armoured train                   |

A vehicle conceptualised by [Natem Calgori](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Calgori_Natem.md) and
[Anaurin Ettua](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Ettua_Anaurin.md), as a result of baedoorian and
latoka technical knowledge.  
Not soon after first prototypes were produced, [Baedoor Railroad Enterprise](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/Baedoor_Railroad_Enterprise.md)
was established, leading to broad incorporation of train systems into Empire's mechanisms.

### Airships
|                  ![](/Assets/Culture/Vehicles/baedoorian_airship_wob.png)                   |  ![](/Assets/Culture/Vehicles/baedoorian_airship_wob_2.png)  |
|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------:|:------------------------------------------------------------:|
| Baedoorian airship depicted in [Wastelands of Baedoor](/Mechanicum/Walkthroughs/WoB/WoB.md) | Two airships flying near each other in Wastelands of Baedoor |

Airships are one of the finest baedoorian vehicles, used widely both for transport of goods and
people, as well as military. Due to their big size and heaviness, their combat usefulness is
somewhat limited, yet due to various modernisations they are also very resilient to various
attacks.
