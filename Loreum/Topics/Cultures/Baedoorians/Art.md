# 🎭 Baedoorian Art

### Baedoorian Avant-garde Theatre
The avant-garde art was a direct effect of more revolutionary movements appearing in NE, notably
[Neuvea Romanai](List/Neuvea_Romanai.md) and political art criticising the industrialisation and
its effects. Its appearance on thoroughly traditional medium such as theatre is credited to
tri theatres, however ultimately it is difficult to point particular single influence that
gave birth to the idea.

Avant-garde theatre was critical towards industrial society, and used variety of ways to convey
its message, from mimicking or magically recording sounds of the environment, operating on silence,
frequent fourth wall breaking, scandals, irony interlaced with cynicism, to absurd humour.  
Each theatre applied those mechanisms differently, making the ways these interact with each other
unique mark of director or theatrical community. A rather unique aspect of avant-garde movement
is the fact that actors frequently were partial owners, diminishing the idea of hierarchical divide
within the institution.

A lot of these forms appear later in **machine music**, a genre that is considered inspired by 
the work of avant-theatre masters. The list of influenced art fields and political movements
are however much broader, as most of the new arts construct a circular ever expanding community.

Some most known avant-garde theatres include [Oxiai Theatre](List/Theatre_Oxiai.md), 
[Si Vai](List/Theatre_Si_Vai.md) and [Theatre of Grey Mask](List/Theatre_of_Grey_Mask.md).
Acts such as [Eye of the Chaos](List/Theatre_Oxiai.md#eye-of-the-chaos) became an influential
part of the wider movement.
