# 🏺 Baedoorian Culture
Baedoorian culture is outcome of many historical processes and can be seen as amalgamation of
various influences - primarily jitadian and nomathic.

### Eastern, Western and Ansurian Baedoorians
While even within eastern baedoorians one could claim differences between particular areas
(happening because of nomathian influences and various material conditions and families' reigns),
after establishing Evros and New Baedoor, baedoorian culture split the most significantly due
to sheer distance between areas. This only got emphasized after Empire's fall, pushing differences
further.

While **eastern baedoorians** were always somewhat independent rebels, over time they became what
they tried to escape from - a proud, rooted culture that values their traditions and earned 
experience.  
This took different angle from their jitadian heritage, pushing less focus on community or faith
and more on science, economy and social systems (such as semi-democratic systems). This culture
also seen travels and expansion as means to establish reign and knowledge.

**Western baedoorians**, on the other hand, pushed the idea of expansionism to the extreme, but
also turned it inwards. Their expansion is built on science and trade, but happens through complex
economic nets, relationships of investors and technology.
Socially, westerners emphasize freedoms of entity and a wide array of places and activities 
available for leisure. Equality is seen as something inherently needed, albeit the practice 
often turns this to be heavily performative since a lot of human dignity can be turned upside 
down if it doesn't work within the capitalistic systems.  
That said, the general character of westerners tends to be more welcoming to immigrants and also
experimental, weird art or philosophies, making it culturally diverse and fluid.

In this scheme of things, **Ansurians** land somewhere in between - they inherit a lot of traits
of both groups, emphasizing mercantile focus, need for independency, love for tradition yet openness
for technological progress. Neither of those get to extremes however, as being located in peripheral
side, ansurians tend to assimilate new ways slower, taking needed time to properly accomodate it
in their natural routines. In a sense, lesser position on political map allowed Ansur to thrive
in ways more friendly to its community.
