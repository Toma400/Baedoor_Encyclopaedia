# 🏺 Colourful Literature

---
Colourful literature includes various series created by the publishers to promote reading
and have books be more friendly for travelling. Initially started with brown literature novels,
it became wider trend in late **Old Era**.  
Over time, the "literature" term became normalised enough that it coined the colloquial idiom.

### Brown Literature
**[📓 Scribae page](/Scribae/Series/Brown_Literature.md)**

Brown literature came to life with an advent of train travels taken by commoners, soon after
[Great Railroad](../../../Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Places/Great_Baedoorian_Railroad.md)
was built.
The initial series started by novel publisher to promote reading during the travels. Books were 
meant to bring sense of adventure and not uncommonly featured trains early on, bridging the 
readers with the story this way.

Over years, brown literature got some titles with more mature topics and diversified thematically,
still however having the adventurous character and telling rather simple, heroic stories, full
of epic gunfights and duels.

### Green Literature
**[📓 Scribae page](/Scribae/Series/Green_Literature.md)**

The idea of these series came from [Loyd Vertilli](../../../Characters/List/Vertilli_Loyd.md)
being huge advocate for more accessible science. It was inspired by brown literature in form,
however books from green series contained fairly complex explanations of various topics,
considering the still somewhat easy language that Vertilli used.

First books came from **Quarius University**, which was later followed by more universities
that appreciated the initiative.

### Red Literature
**[📓 Scribae page](/Scribae/Series/Red_Literature.md)**

Romance novel series, inspired by the boom of Brown Literature. Its introduction came off as
similarly big success and solidified "literature" series as light books read during journeys.

### Grey Literature
Grey literature is not really a series, but a term used for cheap news magazines. The grey colour
is indicative of them being sold on the streets, with pavement having characteristic colour.
