# 🏺 The Fall of Gods

---
**The Fall of Gods** is a magazine founded in 368 NE, in **New Baedoor**. Released quarterly, it
was known for its scandalous character and critical attitude towards society. It was often
criticised for propagating immoral lifestyle. Despite that, the magazine discussed culture
and its diverse forms, albeit with focus on revolutionary and novel art.

It is one of the current main voices of experimental and avantgarde art of eastern world, only
competing with [Eternal Flame of Avantgarde](Eternal_Flame_of_Avantgarde.md) and [The Age of Decline](The_Age_of_Decline.md).
The Fall of Gods is distributed throughout **West Baedoor**, outside which works popular subscription.
