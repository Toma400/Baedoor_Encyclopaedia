# 🏺 Si Vai Theatre

---
Founded in November 417 NE, it focuses on [avant-garde performances](../Art.md#baedoorian-avant-garde-theatre).
Its performances are heavily inspired by **tri theatre**, distinguishing it from its contemporary
[Oxiai Theatre](Theatre_Oxiai.md).
