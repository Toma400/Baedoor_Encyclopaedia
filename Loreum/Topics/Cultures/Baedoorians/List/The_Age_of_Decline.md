# 🏺 The Age of Decline

---
**The Age of Decline** is magazine that debuted in 322 NE, in **New Baedoor**. Founded by two
**neuvea romanai** thinkers, **Lea Narghot** and **Mira Mounis**, initially the magazine was
distributed across niche philosophical circles, as a way to popularise neuvea romanai concepts
and critique of new order after Empire's fall. Ultimately, as the popularity of magazine grew, it
became more of a platform for art and philosophy to intertwine with each other.

In contrary to [Eternal Flame of Avantgarde](Eternal_Flame_of_Avantgarde.md) and [The Fall of Gods](The_Fall_of_Gods.md),
The Age of Decline remains more holistic, both in terms of inviting classic art, and through
broader topics being discussed on its pages.
