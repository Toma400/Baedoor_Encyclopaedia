# 🏺 New Culture of Baedoor

---
**New Culture of Baedoor** is the longest printed magazine discussing new art and cultural movements,
founded at the end of Old Era and being consistently printed for next three centuries. Being
financed by [Varteford family](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/Families/Varteford.md), it ensured 
magazine's success.

The magazine itself is considered as a cult classic, inspiring many later authors to create similar
initiatives. An example of such inspiration is [Eternal Flame of Avantgarde](Eternal_Flame_of_Avantgarde.md).
