# 🏺 Theatre of Grey Mask

---
Theatre of Grey Mask was founded at the end of 420 NE. It was inspired by previous [avant-garde](../Art.md#baedoorian-avant-garde-theatre)
theatres created in that time, however its direction aimed to rather reflect on everyday life
and the perversity of it.

Actors of the theatre create also a secret society called [Order of the Crossroads](../../../Guilds/List/Order_of_the_Crossroads.md),
being an art-politic movement. Some of its ideological aspects are also conveyed through theatre's
performances.

Unlike many its contemporaries, Theatre of Grey Mask crew is predominantly baedoorian.
