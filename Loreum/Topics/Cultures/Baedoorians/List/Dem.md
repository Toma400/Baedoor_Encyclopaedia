# 🏺 Dem

---
**Dem** (abbreviated as `D`) is coin used in Baedoor Empire before monetary edict in 822 OE that, 
alongside unifying currencies across conquered lands, withdrawn its use.  
Dems were used as cheaper currency to [funts](Funt.md), with exchange rate of twelve dems being worth
one funt.
