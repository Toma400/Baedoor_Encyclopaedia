# 🏺 Funt

---
| ![](/Assets/coin_baedoorian.png) |
|----------------------------------|
| Concept art of regular funt      |

**Funt** is base coin of baedoorian monetary system since establishing its state. It is made out of
silver and has star shape on both sides of the coin. The currency is often abbreviated to `F`.  
One **funt** was equal to twelve [dems](Dem.md), cheaper currency concurrently used in early Empire.
