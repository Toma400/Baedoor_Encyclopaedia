# 🏺 Neuvea Romanai

---
**Neuvea Romanai** is a thought movement, started at the beginning of third century NE in Western
Baedoor. It broke forth partially as a reaction to new world order that appeared after Baedoor 
Empire's fall and nihilism following the event.  
Neuvea's thoughts are commonly shared in various art and cultural magazines, such as [The Age of Decline](The_Age_of_Decline.md),
niche zins and within academic circles, including self-formed groups like [Smoke River Collective](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/Smoke_River_Collective.md).
Neuveist recognition is more grassroots and underground, however some universities roll it out
into curriculums as a showcase of modern thought.

### Philosophy
Neuvea Romanai emphasized on role of destination as a matter forming self, establishing values
as an absolute guide. According to its thinkers, it leads to increase of personal agency through
such clearer direction provided and transcending individual ego.  
Depending on thinkers, there are many distinguishable branches of Neuvea Romanai that emphasise
more focus on individual responsibility, a moral self-obligation, hedonism and joy of existence, 
or importance of faith/belief. The philosophy floats in between sentimentalism and obedience to
feelings, and fairly oppositional utilitarianism.

The more romantic approach, represented for example by [Souvie Darakon](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/List/Darakon_Souvie.md),
is built upon thought that will cannot obey the individual without full digest of emotions in their
whole. Thus, Darakon and her alikes proclaim hedonistic joy of the world as a way to achieve
internal understanding, and radicality towards beliefs as a tool to provide that understanding a
direction and a counterform that does keep person engaged in social and individual life, not
letting the hedonism lead to decadence or apathy.

Utilitarian thinkers such as **Evilar Kegaen** or latoka-baedoorish **Satarap Miritan** consider
the emotional part as one that brings us back to the individual and being too unstable to be
relied upon. In this case belief is a vehicle that should be somewhat examined, and the sentimental
part of faith is somewhat restricted as a potential trap for the self to degrade into its own
ego again. Kegaen does see this as an obstacle in particular in individual-community divide where
egoistic motifs can easily degrade ultimate goal and basically self-sabotate social aspects of it;
while Miritan emphasises more on individual angle and responsibility on internal coherence.

### Name
The name is somewhat a coinage, as *romanai* means "whole being" and doesn't exist in regular
communication. Neuveists (as sometimes philosophers of this doctrine are called) consider it
literally, as their thought is meant to "leave nothing in soul untouched", and even more emphasized
in more extreme angles of thinkers like Souvie Darakon.  
The *neuvea* in name also indicates two understandings of the concept, as in modern baedoorish
it conveys both the idea of "new" and "renewed", pointing at either creating new self, or an
act of restoration.

### Trivia
Neuvea Romanai is heavily built upon ideas of romanticism and existentialism, being somewhat of
them colliding and be a discussion with itself. The transcendence of self and importance of
radical destination of the former confronts the "construct yourself upon self-understanding" 
part of the latter.
