# 🏺 Oxiai Theatre

---
Oxiai (baedoorian transliteration of trish "oiksai", _primal chaos_) was an [avant-garde](../Art.md#baedoorian-avant-garde-theatre)
theatre working in **New Baedoor** in years 411-419 NE. Its founder and main director-writer
was [Ainji Otenhat](../../../Characters/List/Otenhat_Ainji.md).

### Iconography
Oiksai, trish for "primal chaos", was a name used for the theatre after its first play of
[Eye of the Chaos](#eye-of-the-chaos), authorial act that made the theatre known in wider 
community thanks to its covering in [The Fall of the Gods](The_Fall_of_Gods.md).

Oxiai theatre since has used sign depicting the eye opening with rays coming out of it in all
directions, creating an abstract symbol with somewhat magical meaning. The iconography is
commonly referred as having roots in Lai-Kine, however it was never confirmed by the theatre's
crew.

### Eye of the Chaos
Eye of the Chaos (tri. _Onvoi na Ksai_) is first dramatic act written by Ainji Otenhat. It
revolves about being part of the society enabling atrocities, from poverty to the military
terror, all narrated in absurd tone.  
Its form combined classic baedoorian theatre with experimental angle, setting the bridge
between two visions seen commonly as opposites. The play has been acclaimed as setting new
standards for the movement. It has been covered by variety of magazines and has made Oxiai
theatre known in wider community.
