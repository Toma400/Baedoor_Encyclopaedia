# ⛵ Arennan

**Arennan** is a vesel active in late Old Era and early New Era, within **Baedoor Empire**
merchant navy.  
At first destined to travel in between **Ontero** and Tharie, as a part of **Thon**'s family
fleet, it got bought by Xavier family in second century of NE and rerouted to travel in between
[Old Baedoor](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Baedoor.md) and
**New Baedoor**.
