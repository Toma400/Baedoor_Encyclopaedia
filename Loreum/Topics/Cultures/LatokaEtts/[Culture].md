# 🏺 Ett Culture
Etts are culture within [latoka](../Latoka/[Culture].md) living on the highland and mountainous
areas of [Rossevette](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Rossevette_Islands.md).
Characterised by their harsher environment, they remained for long time seen as a more primitive
side, focused on survival instead of proper evolution.  
They were considered a nuisance due to frequent raids on latoka and voitri settlements to gather
food, which ended with voitric occupation of Hinnut lands, when Fifth Hand's leaders established
strict guard over their forts and penalized any similar efforts.
In effect, Hinnut etts became even so poorer and most of the settlements have much smaller 
populace nowadays with minimal political or military agency.

### Origins
Ettic culture originates from latoka's Ett clan that had reign over Dewehr Mountains and Ilumat
Highlands on the earliest noted times. This marks them as the oldest latoka culture to stand with
little to no changes, and explains also traditionalism that follows ettic culture.

The name itself also brings etts together as community - one could consider it as a symbol of
Ett king being a father of them all, and by that extension etts being his children. It's further
emphasized by somewhat democratic format the culture is forming its rules and how tradition of
kingship is built.

### Administrative system
Etts, despite being technically spred under two realms, obey the central authority that is
the ettic king. The king is the institution that was kept its family ties to the ettic clan, but
in ways that are somewhat artificial. Marriage institution prioritises connection to the Ett
surname, meaning that no matter who marries member of Ett family, they must take the clan surname
and it becomes its identity continuing further.  
That system made Ett clan much larger and let it not die despite centuries passing, and this
also preserved the bloodline basically making impossible to eliminate entire clan by any hostile
means, earning a title of "ettic mafia" by some more bitter voitric occupiers.

The system however restricts that anyone married from foreign surname cannot be a king, meaning
that in such marriage only the child could bear that title.

### Relationships
Unlike regular latoka, etts weren't seen as equal by voitri, resulting in worse treatment from
the occupants. Similarly, ettic states were only partially freed during the peace treaty, bringing
instability to the region with power coming from multiple sides.
