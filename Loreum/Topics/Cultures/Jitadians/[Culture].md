# 🏺 Jitadian Culture
Jitadians are old descendants of Dyalnesi tribe who were exiled from their homeland and wandered north,
to fertile lands of what is known today as Jitado island.  
While jitadian culture seems to be a monolith to outsiders, there are certain features that diversify
it within - most popularly it can be split into three areas:
- southern half of the island is lived by progenies of Dyalnesi, and so inherited very family-based,
  marine-focused life; they are main providers of food to the state and value their tradition that
  they view somewhat superior
- western coast is the poorest side, most influenced by mesinpeans, but also having difficulties
  after multiple raids; that being said, western coast also holds very productive industries and
  is home to many technologists
- northern half and islands are inhabited by more modern jitadians, with stronger influence on
  technology and enthusiastic about trade, expeditions and science
