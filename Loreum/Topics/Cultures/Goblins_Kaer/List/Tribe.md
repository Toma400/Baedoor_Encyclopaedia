# 🏺 Tribe
Goblins are individualistic, yet tribe-oriented culture. They value ability to know as much as
it is needed for them to survive in good conditions, but that is always filtered through the
mutual dependency to the tribe, as a core construct of goblin culture.  
The relationship is practical: each goblin works towards the good of their tribe, but in exchange
the tribe protects and helps its members.

### Decision processes
Every adult goblin is able to take part in discussing important matters for a tribe. No voice
is considered as higher, however depending on context some tribe members can be found as more
impactful than others, be it through reasonable arguments, experience or non-formal authority.  
The discussion usually rely on rotating the allowance of voicing out the opinion, with a moderator
role taking care of proper process.
As the tribes are usually rather small, disturbances are not frequent, and discussion themselves
are done rather rarely.

### Rules and ostracism
While the rules of each tribe differ and are not held formally, there is underlying idea that
the goblin is judged upon their function and relationship to the tribe. Meaning that every member
is required to work and behave to their kind in a way that doesn't need resolving by a moderator.    
If goblin misbehaves, moderator is the first instance that deals with that issue, trying to find
a way to resolve it in a way satisfying both parties. That said, it is upon his judgement to
decide whether the goblin should be exiled. In more controversial cases, the discussion is held.

### Gender-blindness
While goblins physically represent two sexes, their culture does not have the concept of gender,
providing more individualistic approach to areas where gender could be in play.  
This means that goblins do choose their partners based on individual attraction, and that goblins
are freely choosing the work they do without limiting themselves to particular tasks.

### Romance
Goblins choosing their partner is based on physical attraction and potential psychic traits, which
are the two factors they care about. As goblins are gender-blind, the choice is not dictated by
their prescriptive scheme, but the patterns can repeat from pure biological reasons.  
The relationships held by goblins are not formal and seen more as a partnership than something
particularly sacred. That said, goblins tend to be faithful once established. The relationships
are usually monogamic, however poligamic ones appear from time to time.  
Relationships are aimed on helping each other and enjoying the time with each other, including
more intimate character of the relation, but it is not seen as something unique or limited to
it. Goblins who can't mate with each other search freely for one willing to do so, as this is
not seen as part of romantic life.

### Work & care
Children, once born, are raised in family only for the short time of them being dependent
on parents; the moment they learn enough behaviours, they are sent to schooling area where they
sleep and learn all basic skills, being taken care of and teached by carers. This ensures new
goblins have all what's needed for them to be useful both for themselves and the tribe in the
future.  
During adult life, goblins take care of themselves rather individually unless not being able to,
but they can count on tribe system to provide them food, area to sleep, tools, healing and overall
safety. In return, every adult goblin is required to work and is judged heavily on that basis.  
Goblins do not treat disability as a reason for adults to not work, however such entity preferably
should adjust their work to be able to utilise their capabilities the best. The view of goblins
using other limbs to overcome disabilities is not unusual.  
Goblins who are unable to work for temporary reasons (such as being hurt) are taken care of by
healers, although if their inability is permanent, depending on tribe they might be exiled.  
Even more rigidly, diseased or possessed goblins tend to be exiled outright, and children who
are known to have issues preventing them from being useful for the tribe are often left to die
or offered to the more bloodthirsty spirits. 

### Goods and their distribution
All goblins work towards the good of the tribe, and the effects of their work are accumulated
in tribe's storage (depending on case it can be protected hole in the ground, cave, building)
with maintainer being the one who takes care of its quality since then.  
Maintainer is also a trusted role that distributes the storage good to goblins, in roughly equal
manner and in a way that reflects their needs. The surplus is either stored or traded.  
Each individual goblin can have their personal property, but uses storage for resupplying what
they can't produce or to get daily rations of food. That's why storage and cooking area is often
set up near each other in goblin camps and villages.

### Trade
Goblins primarily trade only with other goblins. The trade system is managed by tribe's maintainer
and done primarily on surplus products that will not affect tribe's conditions negatively.  
Goblins do not allow trading with other races, however in practice it is more nuanced - kehts
and erds can trade with goblins, albeit this process is discussed within the tribe beforehand.  
In very rare instances an individual can also be allowed to trade with the tribe, however they
must prove their trustworthiness and need, and only after that the discussion over the decision is
held.

### Migration
Goblins do not migrate between tribes, sticking to the one they were born in. The exception to
it is if the tribe is on the brink of extinction, counting only few members, in which case
the ones left can search for the tribe to join, in order to survive.  
Similarly, exiled goblins can search for another tribe to adopt them, however single exiles are
usually distrusted as there might be a good reason they were exiled for important reasons.

### War
One of skills trained during early years of goblins is ability to fight, usually with multiple
types of weapons. They also learn how to craft weapons, so they can resupply arrows or thrown
missiles from simplest materials gatherable in Kaer forests.  
The more skilled goblins can be voted as war chiefs and command either the tribe or its subgroup.  
Another special role within goblin army is architects, who are goblins experienced with building
barricades or other constructions to help the army have better chances to win over the enemy.

### Special roles
Those roles are known to require unusual trust and are chosen during tribe meetings every now
and then.

- Moderator - mediates between parties in everyday life, and controls correct flow of discussion
              during the meetings; it is role voted during meeting itself, with some tribes
              choosing more than one moderator
- Maintainer - takes care of tribe storage, maintaining it correctly and distributing its goods
               evenly; during rare cases of trade it is also a representative of the tribe;
               the role is voted during meetings and as prone to corruptive practices, it can
               be judged way more harshly
- Carer - supervises and teaches goblin children; this role is voluntary, but in case of lacking
          carers among the tribe the vote is cast

### Skilled roles
Those roles are just a normal performed work by goblin kind, but require skills that are not
common among the folk. This means that usually there is only one or two goblins actually doing
such work, making their importance in tribe more important.  
Due to that however these roles are highly valued and aspired to, meaning goblins are willing
to terminate and learn skills to level up in informal hierarchy. This keeps such roles passed
onto new generations.

- Healer - skilled with healing practices, usually being able to perform surgery and use alchemy;
           in rare cases this may also mean magic abilities coming in addition
- Linguist - knowledgeable with languages of nearby non-goblin races; being able to at least
             communicate between both parties is used as a translator; if linguist can also
             read texts, this can be also ability they can help technician or other goblins with
- Smith - experienced with metal processing and specifically forging metal tools and weapons
- Technician - having better insight into mechanics of various materials, technicians construct
               various devices to help the tribe be either more efficient or better suited for
               conflicts; kaeric goblin technicians are not usually skilled in reading human
               text (unlike baedoorian goblins) and their inventions are usually simpler and
               not as impressive
