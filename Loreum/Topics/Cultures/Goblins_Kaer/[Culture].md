# 🏺 Kaer Goblins Culture
Kaeric goblins are group inhabiting [Kaer](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer.md),
known for their devotion to local spirits and tribal nature. They do not interact much with other
races, and specifically after baedoorian invasion, are cautious or outright aggressive towards
humans.  
Kaeric offshoot characterises itself primarily by more distanced approach and dedication to
spirits that are part of tribe's everyday life. Similarly to other goblins, kaeric ones are
artistic and skilled with simpler technology.
They live in probably the most varied forms, setting camps and small villages, including ones
in caves. Unlike baedoorian ones however, cave settlements do not go particularly deep and are
used primarily to cover from rain and other dangers.
