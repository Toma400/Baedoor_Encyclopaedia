# 🏺 Kaerian Culture
Kaerians (erd. *seri*) are nomathian culture that arose in [Kaer](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer.md)
during late Pre Era, when nomathian ships reached those lands. They slowly expanded their influence
south, but were halted when approaching kehtic borders.

Kaerian culture evolved slightly from nomathian root they came from, but followed pretty much similar
philosophy and leaning.

The dusk of kaerian culture happened with **Baedoor Empire** conquering Kaer. Nomaths were then the
first to be conquered and so hold little to no resistance. Their culture also got heavily baedoorised,
which left their culture in weird state of transition between their heritage and new, modern state when
Empire backed from southern islands entirely.  
The effect of this colonisation is felt to this day with some kaerians rejecting those influences and
proudly calling themselves as "serj", as "nomaths of pure blood" (which usually is not even a true
statement, as many colonisers married themselves into nomath families). The term was previously used
by kaerians to indicate their long history and traditions.

What is pretty special about kaerians is that unlike nomathians they originated from, they kept their
language throughout occupation and use it to this day.
