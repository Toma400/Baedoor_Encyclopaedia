# 🏺 Mirror Cage

---
**Mirror cage** is a glass cube designed to keep bad spirits, nightmares and all other causes
of bad energy caged. They are used universally by pahtris, latoka and goblins.

Mirror cages work as a trap, however require some effort to actually get spirit or nightmare
caught - they are not passive, despite latoka using it more commonly as wish-good decoration.  
Both reflective and normal glass are used nowadays, but only reflective glass has actual use
for keeping spirits.

Breaking mirror cage with caught spirit (or letting them out without skills to keep them in
control) is a dangerous thing and can end up in mage's death, or worse even, a revenge on
community that was a part of the imprisonment.

### Cultural lens
Pahtri perceive the trap through perspective of mirrors as introspective symbol, based on the
idea of reflection bringing you self-understanding.  
Through this lens, pahtric mages often lead spirits into mirror cage for them to devour themselves
by infinite self-reflections. Mirror cages are therefore seen as more deadly, and forbidden to use
in such radical way. Normal imprisoning depends on context, but is considered moral if done as
self-defence.

Latoka see mirror cages more as mythic decorations, and thus don't care as much about material,
believing rather that mere existence of the item in house can keep nightmares away.

Goblins don't use mirror cages usually, but some goblin shamans keep them in case there's any
need to defend the tribe from spirits. They are also more keen on using caught spirits as future
defence slaves, though few tribes that tried so ended up being decimated be enraged spirit
that ran out of control.
