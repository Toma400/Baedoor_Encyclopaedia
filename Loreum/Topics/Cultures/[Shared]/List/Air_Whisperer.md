# 🏺 Air Whisperer

---
A **magitech** contraption, initially originating from [Seiteh](../../../Geography/Seiteh.md), using
spellcasted electricity as origin point for the mechanical parts of the instrument to translate
into emitted sound. Depending on spell and whisperer construction, sounds vary heavily, evoking
different tones and even feel to the electric vibrations. That allows for quite wide flexibility,
incentivising experimentation.  
Air whisperers usually are categorised into more ambienty or dynamic.

### Spread beyond Seiteh
While original air whisperers - and ones true to their sound, heard in [strobe music](../../Ghlodds/List/Strobe_Music.md) -
remain ghloddish, with the interest of baedoorian musicians, there has been many attempts on 
reverse-engineering and experimenting with the technology. The most prominent spread of the 
instrument can be found in **West Baedoor** (where it was accustomed by [machine music](Machine_Music.md)
musicians) and by [kehts](../../Kehts/[Culture].md) for noisier backgrounds of their **throat blessings**.
