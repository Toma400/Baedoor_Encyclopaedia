# 🏺 Machine Music

---
**Machine music** (sometimes also referred to as "factory music") is a new music genre that
emerged in second half of third century NE, in **New Baedoor**. The genre takes inspirations
from [avant-garde performances](../../Baedoorians/Art.md#baedoorian-avant-garde-theatre) and its
use of environmental sounds, silence, as well as distortion and noise.

First machine music acts were partially a performative groups that put bigger emphasis on musical
aspect in comparison to theatres, but tried to present a critical voice following similar ideas
as the new art wave of the time. The sound was heavily based on environment emulation or recording,
combined with metallic noises, as musicians used pipes and other factory objects as self-made
instruments. That usage coined the genre's name, as well as provided symbolic showcase of
ideas of empowering the commoners (often working in factory) and doing so by simple means, available
to everyone and made through individual's work.

Over time, the genre diversified and focused more heavily on music, introducing more diverse
instrumentation. Some musicians fascinated by oriental ghloddish music brought instruments
such as [air whisperers](Air_Whisperer.md), with others inspired by it starting to make cheap
self-made copies of to figure out fitting sounds.  
That let new subgenres to emerge, having more dynamic or depth, and presenting innovative sounds
to baedoorian audiences.
