# 🏺 Kaikhi Culture

Kaikhi are extremely adaptive, so their core culture never stayed constant. Shaped by their
relationship with goblins, erds, talaaxi, kehts and even baedoorians, they always adjusted their
behaviours to fit within the wider society they were part of.  
That being said, kaikhi are not devoid of their own culture, albeit its roots are likely forever
lost and the only somewhat faithful followers of it are found in forest located on [Kaer](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Kaer.md)'s
southern peninsula. Kaikhi living in kehtic cities or outside of their territories tend to follow
local traditions and culture, filtered through their own lens they keep by organising into small
groups. Ghettoisation culture was an outcome of kaikhi being perceived as lesser citizens throughout
the times, arising at the time of Talaaxi occupation.

### Under Talaaxi rule
Once Talaaxi invaded Kaer, Kaikhi withdrew quickly and dealt with Talaaxi to live as lower class
citizens of their newly built empire. Kaikhi assimilated into their culture, following their
traditions, celebrations and working under their rule.  
While technically they were free citizens, they were treated as worse kind even within their class,
and had no possibility to go beyond their status. This forged beginnings of ghetto culture that
Kaikhi are part of to this day.

### In Kehtic state
Kehts liberated Kaikhi from Talaaxi, but they haven't given them back their lands and offered
similar conditions of living as free citizens in the newly built civilisation. This title however
meant much more under kehtic rule, as Kaikhi were offered much less oppressive reign and more
equal treatment.  
This led to Kaikhi embracing more of remainders of their own culture, bringing some of their roots
back into their everyday life. That said, Kaikhi still were using assimilation technique to better
fit the kehtic society and so that made kaikhic culture be also heavily influenced by kehts.  
While Kehts treated them much better, the portrayal of Kaikhi were still as somewhat lesser than
the hosts. This is the reason why the ghetto culture is continued among the Kaikhi, this time
however forging stronger bonds with them maintaining their own culture without worries.

### Southern Kaikhi
The exceptional group of Kaikhi are residents of the forest found in southern Kaer's peninsula.  
While their culture also derailed from its origins, it is seen as the most faithful due to
those Kaikhi surviving almost intact throughout the millennias - in return, paying the price of
extreme servitude to the mad spirits that haunted the forest Kaikhi reside in.  
The assimilated Kaikhi often fear the southern kind, as they do no leave their area and are seen
as unpredictable, chaotic or just mental. The mixture of spirits and inhabitants being haunted
successfully provided them relative peace, as even Talaaxi feared the madness, being 
superstitious beings.
