# 🏺 Ormath Culture
Ormaths see themselves as maintainers of [Arennan](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Arennan.md)
and "spirits of the flesh" (however metaphorically).

### Nomadism
Much like ormaths see wind spirits as travellers, they do perceive their own nomadic character as a way
to partake in the mission good spirits do.  
Ormaths know about hidden river caves in Arennan deserts and cultivate their flora, which is one of
many reasons they travel from place to place. They maintain the cave, in exchange taking its resources,
such as water and plants. The locations of caves are known only to ormath shamans and sometimes elders.

### Clan & Hierarchy
Ormaths are organised into clans, each of them having different, yet similar structure. They are ruled
by elders, with shaman taking special place of being a semi-authoritative leader.

### Architecture
Ormaths live in leather tents, made on circular base.

### Art
Ormath artistry is done on two levels - they tell stories of spirits and centuries through cave paintings.
Meanwhile, there's also material culture where ormaths depict more everyday themes, making them into
abstract geometrical patterns in fabrics.
