# ⚗️ Saphtric Technologies
Saphtri technology is built heavily on kinetics, with its engineering primarily studying properties
of matter, specifically ones that are used for its motion.

### Flying Ships
Saphtric flying ships are one of the finest crafts of their technology branch, built in a manner that
uses air to make them fly long distances with great control and no fuel needed, as well as cheap cost
of building each aircraft. That said, they require a lot of skill to construct and fly, and so saphtric
sky technologists and pilots are highly valued.  
Flying ships are built in minimalistic manner, with focus on aerodynamics supported by sleek design
utilising as much wing power as possible to let the pilot fly on long distances by smart usage of
gliding mechanics.  

Saphtric flying ships are usually incredibly small and ranges from tiny construction to be equipped
by person, to only a bit bigger construction to be used by up to two people. The bigger ship gets,
the more it tends to lose on its manoeuvrability, so the choice is often measured by use case.  

There's no fuel used by those ships, and their entire power is given to them by the launcher that
pushes them up to the sky. Once there, the pilot must keep the pace of flying, as once they land
the ship will be unable to go back to the sky without another launcher push.  
Since the flying ships are operated purely by their own initial boost, pilots (especially on war
operations) often mount some ways to get additional boost in critical situations. This can help
inexperienced newcomers, but is rather used by pilots to escape a crash, extend their fly or rescue
themselves from other risky situation.  
Also, some newer construction implement additional ways to control wings, letting them increase
the speed and so extend the flight. Most of those mechanics were invented during late Old Era as
enhancements needed for saphtris to sabotage the occupants from above.
