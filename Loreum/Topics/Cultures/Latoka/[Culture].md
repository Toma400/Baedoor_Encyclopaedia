# 🏺 Latoka Culture
Latoka culture is built upon the values that merge their individuality and societal bonds,
emphasizing familial and private freedom, together with obligations towards the local community.

Latoka consider community as elevating factor, thus work done towards that community is highly
valued. Latoka do not differentiate between physical and mental work as strictly as other
cultures. The physical work is valued with the highest respect, as considering frail bodies of
latoka, each job demanding strength is seen as particularly valuable. Tools themselves become
an important part of the culture in such equation, standing as equally valuable to books.  
Latoka put emphasis on determination, organisation and logic, meaning that as much as pure
work is value in itself, the effectiveness and reasoning is important perspective through which
it is seen also.

Latoka's orientation on practicality and trade brought them huge respect among industrialist
cultures of the world, particularly baedoorians who treated them as most equals among all
conquered races.

### Working Classes
The equality of work means that farmers, masons and miners are seen as huge contributors to
society. Dirty jobs such as waste gatherers, medics and carers are also seen, amassing fairly
reasonable working class awareness among the kin.  
These two groups are considered a "providers" and "maintainers", reflecting the most respected
groups.

Traders, couriers and carriers are considered "moving" jobs, and they are jobs latoka most excel
with thanks to their agile bodies, technological advancements and intelligence bundled with
charisma. These jobs, while not as heavily respected within latoka themselves, are popular option.
It is an image through which most foreigners know latoka.

Organisational jobs are respected through lens of ensuring quality and proper processes, but
the judgement of the effects is what leverages the power of these jobs over the field. There's
no inherent status connected to it that wouldn't need to be earned, and so most latoka get
to the top positions through mundane, increasingly more valuable work from the grassroots.

### Transport
Latoka are known to be effective inventors of various transport systems, helping them to traverse
their jungle homeland. Being knowledgeable of technology, they used it either in itself or with
combination of magic to achieve faster systems to move around.

Simplest of latoka transportation devices are boats - from small and fast worker rafts that were
able to travel rivers, to bigger ships used for journeys across Rossevette islands and outside.

| ![](/Assets/Culture/Vehicles/latoka_train.jpg) | ![](/Assets/Culture/Vehicles/latoka_train_2.png) |
|:----------------------------------------------:|:------------------------------------------------:|
|          Rough sketch of latoka train          |         Latoka train concept by SebaPRC          |

One of the finest transport tools latoka invented was train systems. Initially being rather a simple
devices moving on steam engine power, their concept became much more advanced thanks to support
of baedoorian noble families seeing its potential.  
Eventually, train transport became one of the most beneficial technical collaborations between
the two civilisations. While it yielded much less profits for latoka than it did for newly built
[Baedoor Railroad Enterprise](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/Baedoor_Railroad_Enterprise.md), it led
latoka technicians to improve on the concept to make designs more sleek and fitting the use
their culture needed trains for.

| ![](/Assets/Sources/L_010_Egypt_Colony/arv_rossevette_10.jpg) | ![](/Assets/Sources/L_010_Egypt_Colony/arv_rossevette_13.jpg)  |
|:-------------------------------------------------------------:|:--------------------------------------------------------------:|

A device built as a result of latoka unifying was *teleport*. It was a combined effort of latoka
technicians and mages to create a machine that would utilise force of void to transfer single
entities across areas. Initially it was built only in strategic places and served as a way for
messengers to deliver military information. These teleports included multiple steps to properly
start and protections that were meant to destroy anyone coming without proficient knowledge.
