# 🏯 Latoka Architecture
Latoka architecture varies depending on realm, as latoka never were too homogenous culture in
itself. The styles however do not follow political alignments, being shaped more by material
conditions and historical borders.

### Coastal style
One of the more characteristic and widespread styles is **coastal** architecture, seen across
whole northern coast, from Tarsis to Kastis and ruins of Tehevel. It can also be seen in Karos, as
part of its huge cultural melting pot.
