# ⛵ Veno Edran

| ![Veno Edran](/Assets/veno_edran.png)       |
|---------------------------------------------|
| Veno Edran depicted by skeletontonguedworld |

Baedoorian galleon travelling between [Old Baedoor](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Baedoor.md)
and **New Baedoor**, passing through [Ansur](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon/Ansur.md).  
It sails under **East Baedoor** banner and serves mostly to transport passengers.
