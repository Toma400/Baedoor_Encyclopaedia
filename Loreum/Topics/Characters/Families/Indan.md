# 🎎 Indan Family

Indan was small baedoorian family residing in Baedoor city, mostly being involved in craftsman
trade and rare military operations. Considered as one of the poorer ones, they were seen as a
respectable entity in terms of fostering economy and patriotism.  
Their involvement in Varteford takeover in 994 OE made the family lose almost entirety of its members,
with two descendants emigrating soon after the fight.
