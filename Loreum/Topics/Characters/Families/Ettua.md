# 🎎 Ettua Family

Ettua is family located in [Baedoor city](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Baedoor.md)
that built its importance on heavy industry, particularly operating mines, producing tools, and
later also being the ones leading industrial revolution by producing trains and firearms.  
Has close bonds with [Indan family](Indan.md) and [Xavier family](Xavier.md), though their relationship
with the latter struggled heavily during late OE interregnum and haven't fully restored ever since.
