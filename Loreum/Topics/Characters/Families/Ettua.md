# 🎎 Ettua Family

Ettua is family located in [Baedoor city](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Baedoor.md)
and **Lancres**, a result of marrying [Lua family](Lua.md) to ettic aristocracy. It represents
moderate conservativism, being more open with foreign policies, but still considering national
interest as the most important good.

Initially, yet as Lua, family focused on production of tools and heavy industry, helping other 
families with operating mines and setting up factories. They were also monopolists on peat 
extraction from Lancres' swampy areas.  
Later, also due to ettic expertise on technology, Ettua became one of families leading industrial
revolution by producing trains and firearms. The family also shares ownership of [Baedoor Railroad Enterprise](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/Baedoor_Railroad_Enterprise.md)
with Calgori.  

Ettua has close bonds with [Indan](Indan.md) and [Calgori](Calgori.md) families, though their
relationship with the latter struggled heavily during late OE interregnum and haven't fully 
restored ever since.  
Thanks to marriage with ettic clan, Ettua had also close bonds with **Hinnut etts**. While their
connection disappeared over next century, it allowed for establishing connections with [Ascaron](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Rossevette_Islands/Cities/Ascaron.md)
and mutual exchange of technologies.
