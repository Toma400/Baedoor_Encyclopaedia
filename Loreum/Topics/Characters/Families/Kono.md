# 🎎 Kono Family

Kono family is big family residing in both [Baedoor](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Baedoor.md)
and [Isuga](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Isuga.md), with the latter being
their main area of operations. Their field of interests is primarily farming and textile industry.

Kono left Baedoor city during interregnum affairs, leaving their possessions to Calgori family they
trusted. In late first century of New Era, they came back to the capital.
