# 🎎 Abrahk Family

Abrahk family is the oldest baedoorian family in Alkaesar, reigning over it ever since the city was
built. They control its trade and have their own militia.  
This extensive control over what is formally part of state had Abrahk in several tensions with Baedoor
Kingdom, although with little effort for things to change - especially with how peripheral Vershaton in
general is perceived by most baedoorians living on western side of the island.  

Abrahk has close ties with Skirran's families, Jitado embassy and [Varteford family](Varteford.md)
from western part of the country.
Tom Varteford taking the throne in Old Era also legitimised Abrahk's sovereignty over the city.
