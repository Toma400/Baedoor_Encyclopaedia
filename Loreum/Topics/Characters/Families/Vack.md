# 🎎 Vack Family

A nomathian family dating back to early **Old Era**, originating from **Ontero**, but currently
living also in [Dvickhet](../../Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Dvickhet.md) and 
**Delleret** land strip. Initially known as Wakk, they adjusted their surname as a result
of baedoorisation.

Perceived to be on the lower side of the aristocratic wealth, Vack family originally were known
as rat catchers and providers of transport and travel utilities. Their formal interests took
reasonable hit when the railroad was built.

, but over the time rumours said that they do not
necessarily eliminate them, but rather train as private army. Nothing could be proved so far, but
there were accidents of rats appearing out of the blue in parts of the cities, coincidentally
also impacting other families if they stepped on Vack's toes. Vack family is claimed to have
relationships with smuggling groups that visit Ontero, as well as other coastal mafias, which
could explain how relatively small family still remains relevant for this area.
