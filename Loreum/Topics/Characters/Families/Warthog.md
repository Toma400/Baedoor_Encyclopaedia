# 🎎 Warthog Family

**Warthog** is nomatho-baedoorian family living in Baedoor and Dvickhet, and having small appearance 
in Isuga.  
Their origins date back to the time of nomathian rule of the Island, occupying entirety of western 
and southern coast.

During the conquest of nation to later become baedoorians, Warthog as one of few nomathian families 
decided to abase themselves, letting them be part of newly building society.  
Unlike other families though, they didn't descend into slow decay, but rather embraced their new role 
and tried to earn the respect within baedoorian society.

While their position was contested and considered subpar by many families, Warthogs coined their 
importance in Baedoor and Dvickhet cities. They invested heavily in military, but didn't neglect 
supporting families they found useful to ally with.  
Warthogs strategy was very utilitarian - knowing the most powerful families won't accept the outcast, 
they supported the weaker ones to raise in power and eventually having a debt towards them.

Warthog surprising rise to power came in middle VII-th century, as they became increasingly bigger 
part of Baedoor Empire's army power. With other families supplying only small funds and only Xavier 
investing heavy amount of money into military, Warthog became second biggest investor and also 
leading weapons supplier.  
Firearm factories became Warthog's most powerful economy arm, basically allowing for such a relentless 
pace Empire had in war at its time.

This reliance on Warthog family made them eventually take the throne, with support of patriotic 
families and other groups having interest in either war or Empire expansion.  
Previous political attitude of Warthogs only radicalised after that - being radical supporters of 
colonisation, they started preparation to the conquest of Kaer, building Third Grand Fleet.
Army had their structure reformed and generals gained a lot of power as a result of Empire getting 
more militaristic focus.

As the Grand Fleet reached island of Kaer in 796 OE, the efficiency of changes could be seen. 
The fights were quick and brutal. Warthog didn't care about diplomacy outside their internal 
interests with families, so any opposition in Kaer was met with swift death.
The northern side of the Island was conquered in two months.

As much as relentless their conquest was, Warthog ruling didn't oppress locals more than to crush 
any rebellious attempts. Goblin tribes living in the forests were left without much interference, 
except for getting rid of those occupying mines important for Empire's further progress.  
Next months however set the major setback to Third Fleet conquests. Kehtic and erdic civilisations, 
already getting into better relations after long mediating processes, found a strong reason to unite 
within one banner, also including remaining serj forces residing in Ortaver.  
Their armies settled in Ortaver and in forests, building both official defence and guerilla 
forces occupying various kehtic fortresses. Erdic Sain clan supplied both with their battlemages, 
converting Baedoor successful military campaign into long, exhaustive trench warfare.

With the army getting more demotivated over time and losses not only affecting soldiers, but also 
equipment, Warthog decided to perform radical move - to burn the forest.  
While its effect wasn't too devastating, it had a tremendous effect on morale of both parties and be 
first sacrilegious action Kaer inhabitants can't forgive their occupant.
The war still needed a lot of time for baedoorians to defeat keht-erd-serj forces, but the front 
slowly moved towards south. When reached the coast, the army focused on strengthening the conquered 
areas, being still targeted by guerrillas and other forms of retaliation.  
Eventually, the next wave targeted Khacari, attacking the port of Roda. The destruction made by ships 
was enough for erds to retreat, hoping for peace treaty giving them some form of protection.
As vile as Warthog was, exactly this happened, letting Khacari keep some form of independence while 
keeping most administration under Empire's rule.

In the next decades, Warthogs together with other families built an infrastructure on Kaer and Khacari, 
bonding the local culture more with their influence. Baedoorian rule was met with mixed response, 
as foreigners tend to overuse their power and also break things sacred to cultures they occupied.  
In return though, they brought relatively big stability, both diplomatic and economic.
The opposition factions existed and tried to undermine or sabotage Empire's rule, but at this point 
they had very little success.

In the meantime, in 846 OE, the current Warthog ruler dies, but the dynasty stays in power, as new ruler 
is chosen and the Warthogs are elected again due to previous success.  
Second Warthog rule is considered as fairly peaceful, with Empire focused on establishing their power 
on Kaer and expanding their facilities. There is however increasing tensions among Warthogs between two 
branches of the family - the leader one and one from Dvickhet.  
With second leader passing in 899 OE, his heir knows he needs to rebuild the strength of his grandfather, 
as only that will ensure his family branch can have support of both majority of his family and other 
ruling ones. He proposes an effort to expand the Empire with yet another state - Arennan.

This results in Warthog being again elected as a king, third time in a row.
Chosen as next leader, Orhat Warthog starts planning of the invasion, restructuring Third Grand Fleet 
and building the necessary infrastructure. It took him much longer time than he realised, so many years 
passed entirely on managing this goal.
Orhat had great success at getting the army ready for invasion, but unfortunate illness took him away 
from the world in early age of 37. This resulted in Dvickhet leader of Warthogs following with the 
election, as Orhat had no male heir.  
The change of character in election could be felt instantly, as more family leaders joined the 
elections and Warthog's chances were not as certain as before. Iilvinim Warthog played all the cards he 
had - that his blood is of true patriot, who inherited a love for the country from his baedoorian 
heritage, but a force from nomaths.
Iilvinim promised invading Arennan quickly and with relentless power, unlike his predecessors.

Iilvinim's oratory skills paid out well, as he was elected as next king of Empire.
He also knew he must fulfill his promise, if for anything then for good of his family. 
In between November 918 OE and next year's May, he hastily brought Third Grand Fleet into readiness 
to invade Arennan.  
Next two years are spent on Grand Fleet painfully fighting Arennan forces - while the army was ready 
for another war, saphtri forces were the most challenging for Empire's power, both due to their 
technological advancements and military mentality of this culture. Even with bases on Kaer and Disane, 
this war costed Empire a lot on transport alone.  
In third year of war, Empire finally could settle their forces on Arennan lands fairly safely. 
It certainly helped baedoorian army to push the front further, but haven't made it less of a 
hell - alone on foreign land, with psychotic enemies and dangerous environment, the advantages of 
Empire power felt absurdly inadequate.
Warthogs invested all their possible money and energy to keep the war going, but the situation was 
desperate. Iilvinim wisely tried to exhaust each solution before using another, which resulted in him 
taking various approaches, from taking volunteers from conquered countries as far as to ask much 
bigger funding from certain families.  
That last solution however was a proof for many families that Warthog lost their power completely.
This resulted in many of them conspiring among each other, while officially politely supporting
Warthog rule and expressing mild concerns.

Finally, after almost a decade of fights, Arennan gave up its independence and became part of the 
Empire in 928 OE. As much of celebrative moment it was, that was only a temporary relief for Iilvinim.
Liberation sentiments were still strong among saphtris, making the victory insufficient to keep proper 
control over the country.  
Iilvinim decided to separate part of the army to exclusively keep an order on that desert land, 
called Desert Divisions. This basically made Arennan a Baedoor army training ground.
Even though in a way effective, baedoorian forces were a constant target of various guerilla groups. 
And while official government stance was against those actions, it was an open secret that a lot of 
political bodies supported the liberation forces one way or another.  
Warthog eventually agrees with Desert Division mayor on what will be his last nail to the grave - a 
slaughter of Circle of Blood Swords, group known for their vocal resistance.
While it was meant to awake fear in oppositional forces, it radicalised them even further, including 
enforcing their support among local communities.

Next decades are spent in weird in between state, where there's deceptive peace, at least in formal 
sense. Yet, under that illusory stability, there is increasing internal and external dangers 
boiling - resistant forces of Arennan are a constant nuisance, while in the capital itself the 
discontent among families grows. The conflicting interests no longer can be fulfilled through the king 
and the previously mediated contacts was increasing in tensions.  
In the same time, the Empire was starting to feel the costs of wars, including the costly maintenance 
of unsatisfying Arennan invasion.  
This ends in 992 OE, when Iilvinim is assassinated, formally by one of liberation fighters, but the 
context of internal tensions brings accusations of arranging this to the table of Baedoor families 
infighting. This also starts the long era of interregnum.

At this point the Warthog family was powerless, and they stood back from both power and political 
affairs to the shadows of being lowly involved with anything but interests hold by their members.  
Warthog came into small significance back in second century of New Era, but their main area of 
influence remains to be Dvickhet. They never really restored their power, although they are 
considered as important cogwheel in current East Baedoor military supply.
