# 🎎 Lensati Family

**Lensati** is [Ansur-based](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Moon_Continent/East_Moon/Ansur.md) higher
class family that owns lavender farms and oil distillery concentrated around **Waine**. The family
helds semi-monopoly on exporting the lavender oil products outside the island which brought them
relative wealth.

Notable characters from this family include **Sara Lensati**, founder of [The Blossoming Lavender](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/The_Blossoming_Lavender.md).
