## Names
Latoka names have some common naming schemes, e.g. `In-`, `-t`.  
It's also worth noting that there's common differentiation between genders by assigning
`e` to masculine form and `a` to feminine. Coincidentally this also follows the same pattern
as ghloddish naming does, albeit the position of those wovels is inside name, not at the beginning.
