# 🎎 Vancuerto

---
**Vancuerto** is a mysterious persona living during **Baedoor Empire** occupation of Kaer and
known for revitalising an art of necromancy among many its inhabitants, including baedoorians, which
slowly expanded even outside the island borders to Baedoor itself.

Not much is known about Vancuerto, as being a loner, his influence was spread mostly through his few
contacts passing his works to underground printing presses. His works were quickly made illegal and
Vancuerto himself was wanted by law, never being caught, however.  
His identity is suspected to be baedoorian due to the name he used, but it is unclear if it isn't just
persona he made to coin his status.
