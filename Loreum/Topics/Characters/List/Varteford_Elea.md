# 🎎 Elea Varteford

---
**Elea Varteford** (also *Elea Abrahk*) is baedoorian writer, poet and activist. Born in [Alkaesar](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Alkaesar.md)
in reigning [Abrahk family](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/Families/Abrahk.md), she married [Tom Varteford](Varteford_Tom.md)
in age of 20, after which she changed her surname to emphasize her soul bond.

Elea is known for her activism in support of women and workers, about which she wrote several books.  
Her beliefs were sometimes criticised for lack of radicalism and bringing thoughts contradictory to her
mission, but many found her views as a soothing middle-ground supporting acting without abandonment of
traditional beliefs.  
It's worth noting that Elea's husband supported her beliefs, backing them through some laws passed
during his reign.
