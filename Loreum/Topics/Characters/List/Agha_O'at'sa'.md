# 🎎 Agha O'at'sa'

---
One of the more known writers of [illusion poems](../../Cultures/Ghlodds/Art.md#illusion-poems).
His life is dated to -XIIIth century of Pre Era, but the exact date is not known. Most of his
poems were rewritten, which is how their contents are known to this day. It's unknown however
how much they represent the original wording.
