# 🎎 Anaurin Ettua

---
**Anaurin Ettua**, born in 665 OE, was one of three sons of then-family's head, **Levan Ettua**.

Heavily interested in physics and mechanics, he studied this subject together with [Natem Calgori](Calgori_Natem.md)
in **Calgori University**. This shared passion built their life-long friendship during which they
worked together and exchanged knowledge.

### Train Invention
Anaurin created first prototype of train-like vehicle in 697 OE. Natem then helped him to improve
over the concept, making the first prototype train be tested outside the Baedoor City in 699 OE.  
After failed attempt, they both worked tirelessly to improve on the idea, finally producing the
train prototype that succeed.  
Once passing the test, Anaurin and Natem founded [Baedoor Railroad Enterprise](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/Baedoor_Railroad_Enterprise.md)
which goal was to create infrastructure for new vehicle system. Natem funded the further research
and production, while Anaurin worked with other families to confirm the investment. 

Due to his focus on marketing the idea, Anaurin became the sole icon of the new invention and 
company.
