# 🎎 Terska

---
| ![](/Assets/Races/kaikhi.jpg)  |
|:------------------------------:|
| Terska depicted by Tammilisäke |

Terska is a [kaikhi](/Loreum/Topics/Races/Creature/Kaikhi.md), working as kehtic representative,
having a role as contractor and diplomat in erdic lands.  
His attire is rather rich to showcase importance, although attitude of many erdic officials towards
him would rather suggest it's more of a self-proclamation.

### Trivia
Terska is a cameo of Tammilisäke.
