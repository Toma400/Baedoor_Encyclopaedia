# 🎎 Naevin Manaut

---
**Naevin Manaut** is now-professor of foreign policies in **Sargin**’s university. Born in Sargin in 
late III century, he has erdic roots through mother who migrated to Baedoor and married a baedoorian
man.

His origins are the reason why his primary academical interest lays in erdic culture, which he wrote
two books about. His first work, a doctoral thesis titled "Brief Overview of Erdic Culture", was
written in 411 NE. For his research, Naevin travelled with his mother to Kaer, more precise to city
of Disanteldir, and experienced firsthand the subject of his book.  
The second book he wrote four years after, following next travel to Kaer, this time funded by the
university itself. An outcome of that trip, "Southern Islands", was a book that covered vast amount
of topics related to lands of Kaer and Arennan in a cohesive, pop-scientific manner.

### Literary works
- Brief Overview of Erdic Culture
- [Southern Islands](/Scribae/Books/Southern_Islands_Manaut.md)
