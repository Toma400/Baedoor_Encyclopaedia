# 🎎 Ernest Constantino

---
A prime baedoorian alchemist living in Kaer, living in Old Era. Known for his double encyclopedic
trilogy where he summarises most of Kaer's flora. He was also publishing minor alchemical tracts.

### Literary works
- Great Herb Encyclopaedia of Kaer, part I
- Great Herb Encyclopaedia of Kaer, part II
- Great Herb Encyclopaedia of Kaer, part III
- Great Herb Encyclopaedia of Kaer, part IV
- Great Herb Encyclopaedia of Kaer, part V
- Great Herb Encyclopaedia of Kaer, part VI
