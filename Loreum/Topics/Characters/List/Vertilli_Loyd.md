# 🎎 Loyd Vertilli

---
**Loyd Vertilli** is academic who worked in **Quarius University**. Coming from lesser 
aristocratic family, he is known as a pioneer of psychology sciences, following thought of 
[Salomon Criss](Criss_Salomon.md) and expanding upon his sociological approach to analysis of 
individual.

Vertilli also initiated the idea of [Green Literature](../../Cultures/Baedoorians/List/Colourful_Literature.md#green-literature),
being huge proponent of educating the masses and seeing potential of his local university press 
creating pocket variants of some scientific tracts. He was the editor of first books from these 
series, rewriting various books into more layman language.

### Literary works
- Green Literature, Biological Basis of Psychology
- Green Literature, How to Resolve Conflicts
- Green Literature, Metaphysics
- Green Literature, An Overview of Psychology
- Matters of Mind `wordplay on -matter-, mind analysed biologically`
- The Theory of Relativity
