# 🎎 Dant de Steye (Viktor)

---
**Dant de Steye**, known under pen name of **Viktor**, is one of the more known herbalists of 
**Baedoor Empire**, living between sixth and seventh century of Old Era.

He described hundreds of herbs known to alchemists, including both flora from his mother continent
and found on foreign lands. He is said to be close friend of [Frederico Alexander](Alexander_Frederico.md),
who helped him by bringing new herbs for research. Some said that Dant de Steye was also a companion
on some Frederico's travels.

### Literary works
- Alchemist Tract
- Flora of Baedoor, part I
- Flora of Baedoor, part II
- Flora of Baedoor, part III
- Herbs of Mes Inpea
- Herbs of Moon Islands
- Herbs of Lands Unknown `?`
- On Herbs, Compendium of Herbalist Basics
- Overview of Poisonous Herbs, part I
- Overview of Poisonous Herbs, part II
- Preparing Base of Mixtures
