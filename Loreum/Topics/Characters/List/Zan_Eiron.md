# 🎎 Zan Eiron

---
Born in 982 OE, in Skirran. First son of **Eyd Eiron**. Known for his fencing skills.

In 999 OE, he travelled to Arennan to learn more on military. His comeback ended up in his death due 
to **Unteupta assassination**.
