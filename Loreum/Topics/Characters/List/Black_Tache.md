# 🎎 Black Tache

---
**Black Tache** is a notable killer, known for his dozen of murders done in [Baedoor City](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Baedoor.md)
without getting caught. His nickname comes from a mustache that was seen from beneath his mask, seen
by one would-be victim.  
He was caught in December 216 NE, with supposed help of journalist who aided guards to potential
target. He was executed two weeks later.
