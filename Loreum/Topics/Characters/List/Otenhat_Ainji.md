# 🎎 Ainji Otenhat

---
Born in 381 NE, Ainji was a saphtric immigrant living in **New Baedoor** since 403 NE. He was
founder of [Oxiai Theatre](../../Cultures/Baedoorians/List/Theatre_Oxiai.md) and a writer of 
[avant-garde](../../Cultures/Baedoorians/Art.md#baedoorian-avant-garde-theatre) dramas performed
there.

### WORKS
- 911 - [Eye of the Chaos](../../Cultures/Baedoorians/List/Theatre_Oxiai.md#eye-of-the-chaos)
- 912 - Eloi Fortress
- 912 - Trilogy of Darkness
- ...
- 919 - The Edge
