# 🎎 Hes Alote

---
**Hes Alote** is known as one of more influencial saphtri thinkers. His lifetime falls on 
138-244 OE.

His major contribution to saphtri culture is **Tractates of Truth**, a twenty-chapters tome
discussing morality, essence of the existence, and society's role in it. The book was widely
used across both academics and artists, and received several supplementary works from other
authors over centuries.

### Literary works
- Tractates of Truth

### Notes
Hes Alote is saphtrified name of LE's Hes A'thot. Another difference from LE's entry is using
plural for the book, to emphasize the multi-chaptered format.
