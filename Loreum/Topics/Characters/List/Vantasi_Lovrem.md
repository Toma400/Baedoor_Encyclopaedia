# 🎎 Lovrem Vantasi

---
**Lovrem Vantasi** is baedoorian of jitadic origins. Born in 466 OE in Sargin, he became known as
author of "The Goblin Society" - an influential book on goblins that deepened already sardonic image of
them with how it was written. The titular mountainer goblins were depicted as creatures trying to
primitively emulate civilised cultures.
