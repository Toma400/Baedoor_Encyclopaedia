# 🎎 Salomon Criss

---
Salomon Criss is an academic living in late era of Baedoor. During his life, he was praised for
diverse approach to sciences, combining philosophy, scientific thought and ethics.  
Writing plenty of tracts, he is sometimes considered a precursor to topics such as sociology
and psychology. His thought was later expanded by academics like [Loyd Vertilli](Vertilli_Loyd.md).

### Literary works
- The Alchemist: Synthesis of Essences `more of a philosophical thesis on convergence of ideas`
- Philosophy of Life
- Physical Perspective On World `materialism analysed as a tool`
- Star Constellations `focused on societal/mythic representation of cosmos`
- A Philosophic Manifesto
- On Love
- The Right of Life
- Tract on the Freedom of the Being

### LE differences
There are several titles touching history, cartography, mathematics, biology and faith that were
left out from LE, as they do make Salomon feel as too much of renaissance man; these can be 
repurposed, but given how many historians we already had in LE, I think just axing them suffices.
