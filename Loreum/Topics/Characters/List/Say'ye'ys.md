# 🎎 Say'ye'ys

---
A green dragon settled in [Ghe'ar Ethr cave](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Seiteh/Lands/Ghe'ar_Ethr.md),
having characteristic green scales. It is friendly towards the city built in his cave, as well 
as [the cult](/Loreum/Topics/Religions/Say'ye'ys_Cult.md) around his person.

### Name
Say'ye'ys is simplification built upon three ghloddish words:
- Sa'e - melody, song
- Sah'r - strong
- She - being, entity

Say'ye'ys can be then translated literally as "being of strong melody", or more allegorically
as "wise being", "being of strong character".

Changes of the name across linguistic eras:
- sa'e'sh're / sa'e'e'sh (first generation)
- sa'y'ye'ys[h] (second generation)
- sa'y'ye'ys (third generation)
- say'ye'ys (fourth generation)

### Trivia
Say'ye'ys is a cameo of Sajevius (or Sajevi), one of the close friends Toma met early on during
his MCreator modding. His avatar featured green dragon, and with exceptional modding skills, Saj
could be seen as a wise sage of his craft.
