# 🎎 Natem Calgori

---
**Natem Calgori**, born in 664 OE in [Baedoor City](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Baedoor.md),
was one of more influential heads of [Calgori family](/Loreum/Topics/Characters/Families/Calgori.md)
in this era.

He was attending **Calgori University**, studying physics. During studies he met [Anaurin Ettua](Ettua_Anaurin.md)
with who he later co-invented train and founded [Baedoor Railroad Enterprise](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/Baedoor_Railroad_Enterprise.md).  
Natem invested a lot of family's money to ensure the new invention becomes a success.
