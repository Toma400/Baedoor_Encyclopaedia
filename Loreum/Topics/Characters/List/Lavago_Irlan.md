**Irlan Lavago** is master of [Followers of the Bound](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/Followers_of_the_Bound.md),
ansurian guild of mages. Promoted to the position in 426 NE despite his relatively young age, he was 
accustomed to ways of FotB works and could excel many other members with his skills.

Irlan practices in the guild include experimenting with ghloddish sigils.
