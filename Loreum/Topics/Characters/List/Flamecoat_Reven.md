# 🎎 Reven Flamecoat

---
**Reven Flamecoat** was a governor of Baedoor between 1024 OE and 21 NE. He was brought to power
after the death of the last king and served as sort of intermediary leader throughout the crisis
leading to Exodus event.

He is considered fatherly figure in modern Baedoor given his care for the Empire to remain
stable.
