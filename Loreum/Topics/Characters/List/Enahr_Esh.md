# 🎎 Esh Enahr

---
<span style="color:#8F3DA5">**universe**</span> [✦](/Loreum/Tags.md#-universe)  

---
**Esh Enahr** (ghl. *E'e E'nahr'r*, Guide of Void's Light) is pseudonym of starshifter who encountered
universe later called as **Uru'ai**, in 272 PE.  
Their journeys took five full days, after which Esh wrote several pages, noting all details they
saw and remembered. Those notes were later copied to **Starshifters** archives.  
Esh's gender or origin are unknown. The "name" in itself does not exist in exact form in ghloddish
naming system, as it is most likely made-up pseudonym.
