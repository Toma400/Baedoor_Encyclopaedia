# 🎎 Joeseh Thon

---
Baedoorian aristocrat and army commander who migrated to Arennan early. He served in many military 
campaigns, and also hold a service of training master in guard hall in Tharie.

Died during [Unteupta assassination](/Loreum/Topics/History/List/Unteupta_Assassination.md) in 999 OE.
