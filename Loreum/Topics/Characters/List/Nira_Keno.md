# 🎎 Keno Nira

---
**Keno Nira** is a scientist and librarian in **University of Illusion**, specifically interested in
astronomy. They give lectures to students from time to time, but prefer performing experiments alone.  
In addition to several books concluding their research, Keno also writes poems.

### Trivia
Keno is a cameo of Xeno, a friend of Toma.
