# 🎎 Two Keys

---
**Two Keys** is a member of [Ansur Thieves Guild](../../Guilds/List/Ansur_Thieves_Guild.md) whose
pseudonym is taken from his extraordinary skills in opening locks. He is close and trusted hand
of [Ferves Navikon](Navikon_Ferves.md).  
He is known to have handmade tool resembling two keys that allows him to check against any lock 
and open them seamlessly, though he does reserve that only for most important goals as for his 
lock picking skills to not deteriorate.
