# 🎎 Frederico Alexander

---
One of the more known adventurers and geographer of Old Era, living between 641 and 722 OE. Being
a citizen of **Baedoor Empire**, he was travelling across most of the known world. Being a writer,
he wrote a great amount of books on his travels and observations.

One of the characteristic aspects of Frederico's approach is very subjective lens through which
he treated the visited lands. It wasn't particularly arrogant or dismissive, but rather one coming
from the idea that whatever area visited became his own in a way. Such solipsistic approach made
his writing equally loved and hated, some describing it as a baedoorish bias, though many 
disagreed saying it's more of a personal character Frederico ascribed to various places.  
This approach is visible by some names coined by Frederico (Nordia, Qucari, Karos being named an
archipelago) and being used in his works.
A case of Qucari is particularly ironic, as the name Frederico hoped the administration to pick up
from his work wasn't used, but the island of Kacari was indeed renamed during Empire's rule, 
just to a different name, Martingo.
