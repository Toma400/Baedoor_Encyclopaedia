# 🎎 Eduard Allaut

---
General of **Desert Divisions**, known for his faithfulness and experience in battles.
Born in 923 OE in [Isuga](/Loreum/Topics/Geography/Baedoor_Continent/Baedoor/Cities/Isuga.md), 
he was teached by his father, general [Isehir Allaut](Isehir_Allaut.md). Being eighteen years old, 
he joined the army.

Eduard Allaut is known mostly from commanding Baedoor army during peace, and for his two 
philosophical-military tracts: *Peace, War and State Matter* and *Path to the Glory: Almanac of Tactics*.
He was a pacifist and propagator of ethical war, which he frequently proved on the battlefield and in
both mentioned writings.

In 999 OE, miraculously salvaged from death in **Unteupta explosion**, he resigned from the position, 
paying tribute to the dead. His death came twelve years after, in his homely recess in Isuga.
