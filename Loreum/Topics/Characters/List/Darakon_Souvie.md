# 🎎 Souvie Darakon

---
**Souvie Darakon**, born 344 NE in West Baedoor and died in 418 NE, was a poet and philosopher, 
known for being a part of [neuvea romanai](/Loreum/Topics/Cultures/Baedoorians/List/Neuvea_Romanai.md),
romantic movement getting traction between third and fourth century of New Era.  
While not the most known from the movement, Souvie's approach to the neuvea ideas were unique, as
it emphasized a love of fate and radical focus on faith in beliefs held. This angle brought 
her a lot of followers who found it more relatable than more rational and unsentimental concepts
of some other romantics of the movement. She is cited as inspiration by e.g. [Smoke River Collective](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/Smoke_River_Collective.md).
