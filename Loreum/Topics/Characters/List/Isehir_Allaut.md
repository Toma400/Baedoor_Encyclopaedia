# 🎎 Isehir Allaut

---
General of Baedoor army. He lived in 895-962 OE. His service fell during early Arennan invasion, 
which was event that both brought him promotion, but also changed him radically from supporter of the 
conquest to outright opponent.

Father of [Eduard Allaut](Eduard_Allaut.md).
