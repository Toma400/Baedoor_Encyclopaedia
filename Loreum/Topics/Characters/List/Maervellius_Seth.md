# 🎎 Seth Maervellius

---
The only chronicler of the oldest times of **Baedoor**, even before it became an empire. His life
falls for 24-113 OE, meaning he could document most of the origins of the state and nation.  
Historians of Baedoor owe him not particularly accurate, but still priceless notes on events
and culture of that early period.

His name is believed to come from ghloddish, as his family represented migrants who came to Jitado
two generations back and marrying into Maervellius family, retaining their customs, however.

### Literary works
- Chronicles of Baedoor, part I
- Chronicles of Baedoor, part II
- Thesis on the World
