# 🎎 Ibrahim vân Buqan

---
Historian of late **Baedoor Empire**, writing on events current to his living (910 - 992 SE) as well
as trying to analyse the past that led to the chaotic moments of the present. A proponent of
more active and holistic ways of research, such as travelling, archeology and humanistic
approach to sciences.

His book on end of the world is considered as semi-prophetic being written in time it was, as it
discussed theories not popular at the time, but ones that seemed to come true with the future
events of **Exodus**.

### Literary works
- Archaeological Theology
- Baedoor, Part I: The Genesis
- Baedoor, Part II: The Middle Era
- Baedoor, Part III: The Pariah Dynasty
- Baedoor, Part IV: Warthog Conquests
- Baedoor, Part V: The Current
- Before Era, On How The Time Is Measured
- Prophecy of the End of the World - Historical Analysis
