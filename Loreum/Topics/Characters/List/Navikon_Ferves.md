# 🎎 Ferves Navikon

---
**Ferves Navikon** is old lady with exquisite skills with revolvers, two of which she keeps always
near herself.  
She is current (924 NE) chef of [Ansur Thieves Guild](/Loreum/Topics/Guilds/List/Ansur_Thieves_Guild.md).
