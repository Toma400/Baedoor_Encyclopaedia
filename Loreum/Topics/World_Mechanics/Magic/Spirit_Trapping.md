# 🎆 Spirit Trapping

**Spirit Trapping** (also sometimes _Soul Trapping_, especially in context of trapping spirits of
lesser living beings) is act of trapping [living spirit](Spirit.md) in a [vessel](Vessel.md) of
adequate or bigger size.  
It's an old practice that is performed to either protect mage (or their environment) from spirit,
enslave spirit to perform mage's will, or to extract living energy out of spirit.

The practice itself is seen as ambiguous ethically and can be seen in negative light depending
on magic culture. Trapping ritual is fairly dangerous, and there are mages who also harness spirits
to their effective dissolution.

### Protection
The most common practice of spirit trapping is with the purpose of protecting oneself or someone's
environment. Many less institutionalised races (such as creature ones) perform spirit trapping
as a form of defence, using various items for it, one of more effective examples being [mirror cages](../../Cultures/[Shared]/List/Mirror_Cage.md).  
Some ways of protection can be also drastic, such as [pahtric](../../Cultures/Tri_Pahtris/[Culture].md)
mirror magic that traps the spirit to its own self-devouring. Usually that works only if the
spirit was aggressive enough (as mirror reflects intentions), but it can be also used against 
harmless intuitions, such as fear or getting lost, yet resulting with the same dissolution.

Trapping however is usually considered a last resort, since it is more effective to convince 
spirits to leave.

### Commandeering the spirit
The second most common reason to trap spirit is to make it serve the mage. Being trapped, it
can basically be used as engine, either for contraptions or rituals. It can also be convinced
to work for mage, with trapping being the proof of his power.

Second option is more chaotic to choose - it might give spirit friendlier approach, as it is
given bigger freedom, but then it can also let it loose more easily.

There are also ways of using the spirit that does not necessarily use its powers explicitly,
but is also not extraction. A good example of it is spirit binding, where spirit being bound
to particular object will replenish its magic energy. That said, binding does give spirit
a way to escape, letting it possess separate (possibly flexible) body.

### Extraction
The darkest reason of spirit trapping is the extraction of spirit. It basically means using the
spirit to grind as much potential resource (magic energy, or some action) for the spirit to
leave out of pressure or be destroyed in the process.

A form of extraction is also magic that destroys the spirit without egoistic motivation. Mirror
magic can diminish spirit, but is done mostly as defence measure.

### Trivia
Spirit trapping can explain Elder Scrolls' idea of "soul gems", as it basically serves similar
usage as other items used for spirit trapping. There's a nuance that unlike soul gems, spirit
traps are not inherently meant to "store spirit to charge items" but the concept of extraction
covers that idea in a way - making Nirn mages a little more exploitative/ruthless/pragmatic.
