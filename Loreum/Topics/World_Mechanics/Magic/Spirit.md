# 🎆 Spirit

**Spirit** as an idea refers to the astral body proper to each living being. It propels the
otherwise inanimate objects.

Spirits can naturally carve [vessels](Vessel.md) in more organic matter, which is how most
living beings are born, and how [tertens](../../Races/Spirit/Terten.md) tend to animate matter
after shaping them, such as leaves, moss, trees or ultimately even dead bodies.

While in theory spirit is a term encompassing one astral concept, and per se spirits don't
differ drastically in essence, there are traits that distinguish some their forms enough for
mages and faiths to categorise them.

### Deific spirits
Deific spirits are ones that can self-contain within themselves, basically work as individual,
self-propelling mini-universe. Some theorise that even the Baedoor universe itself (as also
others) are only an interior of some extremely powerful deific spirit, a creation of its
thoughts.

Deific spirits are the ones that can create vessel without any matter encompassing it, and so
are the most powerful being able to control itself entirely without help of physical matter.
Many deific spirits traverse the Void itself, and are not consumed by it.

Deific spirits encompass deities of various forms, **transparent gods** and **destroyers** alike.

### Higher spirits
Higher spirits include all forms of natural spirits, lesser gods or more powerful, old tertens.
Some count vampires in here too, though that depends on definitions. Higher spirits usually
come from some astral matter before they appeared in the world (hence why vampires being counted
are disputed) and either possess some beings, or remain in astral form only rarely taking
shape. The shape can be both a transformed matter or purely an illusion.

Higher spirits usually aren't beholden to physical world's limitations, being able to transcend
death or connection to body-vehicle; they can also warp the reality rather easily being
still very familiar with **void energy**.

### Common spirits
Regular spirits (or souls) define all living beings with moderate or low connection to 
magic - which includes all races, creatures and animals. They are very bounded to physical
reality, and so their magic is intertwined between the small resources of void energy and 
physical body, with all its limitations.  
This relationship usually leaves most beings not aware of their own magical abilities, and
even less so able to control them. They can express them through masked forms like feelings,
art or ideology, not realising they actively participate in magical process.

Transcending the limitations is one of the main purposes of magical institutions and teachings
in universe, however it is also often seen as faulty to try rejecting the physical to appease
to pure void. Aside of the opposites being not exactly preclusive but rather complementary
(synthesis), the extreme focus on ascending past physicality is seen as dangerous, rushing
the process that in itself lets you assimilate-dissolve in higher realities.

### Ambient spirits
Inanimate spirits such as plants or trees tend to be seen as the lowest in the hierarchy, however
they are rather on different sphere, usually ignoring the other ones and participating in their
own astral circle of life.

Ambient spirits can ascend to higher ones, although the terms on which they do (and if they
were ambient to begin with before ascension) are uncertain.
