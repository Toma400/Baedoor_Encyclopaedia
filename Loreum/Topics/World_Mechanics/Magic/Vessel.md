# 🎆 Vessel

**Vessel** is a term for the astral absence carved in most objects found in existence. By themselves
they absorb void energy, which is transformed into magic during the process.

Vessels vary in size depending on item, its own size and material, and both vessel size and material
of the object might affect absorption rate. This also explains why harder materials tend to be
more difficult to **bind** spell into, but also why they usually take longer to be destroyed
by **unbinding** that works pretty much as interaction between vessel and object itself.

Spirits can enter the vessels, which usually transforms the object astrally, making it more
flexible and absorb more void. It is part of the reason why [spirit trapping](Spirit_Trapping.md)
is not an unpopular way to interact with vessels, even though it is seen as somewhat unethical
outsourcing the work to the enslaved spirit. It also prevents wearing off inanimate objects since
the work is done internally, skipping the "penetration" that binding entails. Internal absorbing
still wears off the material (which is how death is sometimes explained, as reaching final
material fatigue) but does so much slower.  

Vessel regeneration happens when idle, meaning that inanimate objects regenerate their magical
energy slowly, but consistently, and living beings do so during rest, sleep or meditation.

Vessels can also be shrunk or expanded. Death of the living being usually closes the vessel,
eventually making it extremely small, rendering the body as inanimate object and one that does
need expanding to even serve for magical operations.  
Expanding the vessel is a process called **carving**. Carving is a dangerous process in itself,
as it not only requires using void energy on the object to "carve" the absent space, but it also
can easily destroy the said object if mage does not perform it correctly. Carving difficulty
also raises with material, meaning that inanimate tough objects tend to be extremely hard to
have their vessels expanded. Some mages however still try to achieve that, and sometimes breath
life into them - creating magical constructs and golems.
