# 🎆 Magic

---
Magic is a broad word, defined both as energy absorbed from **The Void**, and as a practices
performed through various means that use said energy.  
The plane on which magic operates include both physical and astral one, effectively making it
the main way to interact between each other. This transdimensional form places magic in between
religious and scientific methods.  

Followers of both fields sometimes reduce magic to their own forms of experience the world, 
however magic as a field ranges further than any of them alone. That mindset however sometimes
results in lumping magic as either scientific phenomenon that can be rationalised, or an
aspect of divinity.
