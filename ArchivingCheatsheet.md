# 🧱 Archiving Cheatsheet
Here is the list of all steps that you should care for while archiving notebooks.

### Prerequisites
- make sure the camera you use is not dirty - take clout and go over the camera to ensure the
  photo can turn out sharp
- make photos ideally with bright, yellowish light (usually between morning and early afternoon)
  to provide warm, unshaded feel to photos
  - doing that in cloudy days will result in colder, less saturated photos
  - doing that in later hours can also make windows/objects have longer/patterned shade which
    will make photographing without such obstructions harder
- make sure ideally no object puts shadow on the photographed page
- if you must flatten the surface of the page, try to do it with a way that will not create any
  more shadow than necessary
